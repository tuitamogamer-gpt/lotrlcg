# There & Back Again
A cinematic solo card table and adventure library, inspired by the ancient woodland and illuminated manuscripts of Middle-earth. Original generated Mirkwood background; actual card scans retain original attribution.

Forest-black #101b17, muted parchment #e9e4d6, gold #c4a76a. Cinzel for display titles and Manrope for interface text, self-hosted. Hairline borders, 8px panels and buttons, larger only on card corners. Phosphor icons. Restrained transitions, reduced-motion support. A sidebar anchors the app; a landscape adventure image fills the first screen. Actual hero cards, a prominent embark control, and clear supported content. No marketing filler.

DOM card table for accessible card interactions; canvas only for atmospheric particles. Mouse, touch, and keyboard support. Board zones and phase controls remain clear on narrow viewports. Card details are readable in a dialog. Modal focus handling; descriptive actions; stats always accompanied by accessible labels.
