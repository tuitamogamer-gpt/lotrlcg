# The Drowned Ruins player cards

The ten original designs, 12082–12091, represent 28 physical player cards. Their names, printed rules, OCTGN identities and pack provenance are preserved from the [original RingsDB pack](https://ringsdb.com/set/DR). Dwarven Sellsword and Woodland Courier reuse the existing Collector's Edition implementation; Strider reuses the attachment and passive rules.

| Card               | Implemented rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Argalad            | Exhausts to choose an eligible staging enemy, reducing its threat by Argalad's current attack until phase end. A positive threat reduced to zero causes one damage. Its physical once-per-round limit survives readying, phase changes and save/reload. Engaged and immune enemies are excluded.                                                                                                                    |
| Dwarven Sellsword  | Each physical ally has a separate mandatory end-of-round upkeep. Any player's spendable Leadership pool may pay one resource; otherwise that copy is discarded before new-round resource income.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Dúnedain Remedy    | Normal attachment to a hero offers optional one-damage healing. Its Action spends one resource from the current bearer's pool and transfers the same physical attachment to another legal hero, including a hero controlled by another player. Attachment again offers the healing Response; original ownership is retained.                                                                                        |
| Marksman of Lórien | Entering play by paid play or a put-into-play effect offers a nonimmune enemy in play, including another player's engaged enemy. Each accepted Response reduces that physical enemy's defense by two until round end. Reductions stack, defense floors at zero, and phase changes do not remove the effect.                                                                                                         |
| Battle-fury        | A Quest Action before staging. A controlled ready hero with a legal enemy target exhausts as an additional event cost. A real attack resolves, including eligible Ranged attacks against another player's enemy. Afterward the group may pay one Spirit resource to commit that hero, while it remains exhausted. Declining preserves the attack; canceling the event preserves paid resource and exhaustion costs. |
| Woodland Courier   | Entering play offers progress on an eligible location, including the active location. Forest locations receive two progress; other locations receive one. Normal immunity and exploration rules apply.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Hithlain           | Attaches to an eligible location with a limit of one per location. A Silvan ally played from any player's hand offers one progress on the attached location. Put-into-play entry and non-Silvan plays do not trigger it. Normal exploration discards the physical attachment to its owner.                                                                                                                          |
| Robin Smallburrow  | Actual travel offers any player the option to spend one Spirit resource from their own effective hero pool. Each affected enemy then in play gains engagement cost equal to the traveled location's quest points until round end. Declining spends nothing; simply placing a location as active does not trigger travel.                                                                                            |
| Interrogation      | Targets an eligible enemy with a physically attached Trap. Looks at up to the enemy's printed threat in top encounter cards, independently of current threat modifiers. The player may discard one physical index, including when codes repeat, or return all cards. The remainder keeps its exact original order; an insufficient deck is not replenished from discard.                                            |
| Strider            | A legal hero attachment. With at most two heroes under its controller, its bearer commits without exhausting; with at most five characters, its bearer gains two willpower. These thresholds operate independently and follow the bearer's controller when the card's physical owner is another player.                                                                                                             |

Continuations use serialized effect queues and the normal player-ability boundaries. Tests reload unresolved decisions before selecting targets or payments. Attachments retain physical identities and ownership, and temporary modifiers use the existing phase and round cleanup.

`tests/drowned-player.test.ts` contains 36 semantic checks covering all ten designs, canonical source records, actual public engine actions, cross-player payments and targets, legal target filtering, optional choices, normal exploration, phase/round duration, saved continuations and event cancellation.

`scripts/browser-drowned-players.mjs` exercises every design through real controls at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels. The full suite requires 99 checkpoints, including exact rules text and decoded local artwork for every original face at each width. It reloads pending decisions and checks dialog bounds, horizontal overflow, visible image decoding and browser errors. The report and screenshots are saved under `output/drowned-players/`. `GAME_URL` selects the frozen local build or production URL. `EXPECTED_ENTRY_SHA256` enables strict validation of actual HTTP 200 module response bytes; with it, `EXPECTED_ENTRY` also fixes the expected module path. `RELEASE_SHA` is a caller-supplied report label; remote deployment metadata provides the separate commit attestation.
