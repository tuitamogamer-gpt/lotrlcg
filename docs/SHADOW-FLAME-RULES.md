# Shadow and Flame rules support

The original scenario implements thirteen encounter designs, three double-sided quests and the shared Deeps of Moria / Goblins of the Deep sets. Its physical encounter pool contains 56 cards in normal mode and 42 in easy mode. Setup places one Durin's Bane in staging and sets aside Dark Pit, leaving 54 / 40 cards before the additional setup revelations. Nightmare remains reference content.

## Sources

- [FFG original rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/ffg_content/lotr-lcg/support/rulesheets/Shadow_and_Flame_rulesheet.pdf): difficulty eight, the three encounter sets, considered engagement, independent player attacks, Regenerate timing, Indestructible and unique encounter cards.
- [FFG Easy Mode](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/ffg_content/lotr-lcg/support/easy-mode/LOTR_Easy_Mode.pdf): original orange-ring removals for this pack and its shared sets. Fixtures retain normal/easy per-design quantities.
- [FFG FAQ 1.9](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/1d/bb/1dbb319c-6b1e-466e-9177-75a5857b5cfa/lotr_faq_19_printer_friendly.pdf): Golden Rule, Feint erratum, considered engagement without actual engagement, defending-player identity despite Sentinel, forced effects before responses, uncontrolled encounter attachments and extra attacks outside combat.
- [FFG Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/90/19/90191e4e-a341-4379-b398-5963b7a87ebf/mec01_online_only_rules_reference_for_website.pdf): cancellation preserves paid costs and played status, response timing, actual physical zones, attack resolution, refresh framework, potential change and immediate victory.
- Imported original printed card faces in `src/data/shadow-flame-encounter-cards.json` and `src/data/shadow-flame-quest-cards.json`, matched to canonical OCTGN identifiers and the complete DragnCards database. Each fixture preserves its source URL and front/reverse artwork.

There is an explicit source conflict: the old rulesheet describes preparing one Bane shadow for each initially engaged player, with no shadow for a player whose threat rises later. The imported printed Bane face instead instructs a new shadow for every attack. This implementation follows that printed instruction under the Golden Rule: framework preparation deals none to Bane; each actual normal or immediate attack gets one fresh shadow, which is discarded when that attack finishes. The original insert's older prepared-shadow procedure is not silently combined with the printed procedure.

## Implemented behavior

Setup reduces every player's threat to zero, stages one physical Bane, sets aside the Pit and resolves one fewer complete encounter revelations than the player count. Nearing the Gate advances at nine progress. The Rear Guard advances at sixteen progress or immediately when Bane reaches zero remaining hit points. A hero committed earlier in that round satisfies its end-of-quest four-progress ability even after removal from the quest; the progress bypasses active locations. Last Lord of Moria performs its immediate first-player attack before adding Dark Pit.

Bane stays physically in staging and still contributes four threat. Each living fellowship at threat one or more has its own normal enemy attack and normal player attack opportunities against that same unit. Considered engagement produces no actual engagement triggers. Ranged characters may join another fellowship's attack; other players cannot simply pool non-Ranged attackers. Enemy-attack and player-attack ledgers are distinct. Feint prevents only the chosen fellowship's attack. A late threat increase can produce an eligible attack, and a threat decrease during a started attack does not erase its recorded completion.

Indestructible permits damage equal to or greater than health without destruction or kill rewards. Regenerate heals three after first-player rotation and before Refresh actions. It does not heal again at round end. Nested immediate attacks suspend the original combat and its physical shadows, revealed accounting and damage-protection flags; their new shadow is discarded before the original attack resumes. The complete continuation survives save/reload.

Counter-Spell tests each active attached copy after a paid event is played. The encounter card is discarded without being revealed. A treachery cancels the event's effects, discards that player's remaining physical hand and discards that Counter. Costs and played status remain. A canceled Test of Will or Hasty Stroke resumes the original encounter or shadow effect; Swift Strike's payment and Strength of Will's character-exhaustion cost remain paid. Blanked Counters do not test.

Fiery Sword and Many Thonged Whip are unique encounter attachments, remain uncontrolled and may attach despite the restriction on player attachment plays. Duplicate unique copies are discarded. Sword adds three attack while its text is active. Whip discards a random card from the actual attacked player's hand, including when a Sentinel character defends. Fires in the Deep selects each player's ally before atomic discards, and surges only if it discarded no ally. Its shadow affects only the defending fellowship. Whip Lash includes controlled attachments on enemies and the current quest, while retaining uncontrolled encounter attachments; its Bane shadow discards every attachment controlled by the defending player.

Inner Flame grants its round bonus independently of later attachment blanking. Inner Shadow heals up to five while preserving Surge. The first player may explicitly remove one of their questing heroes to cancel either When Revealed effect. Second Deep blocks direct and buffered progress while Bane has no damage. Ranging Goblin and active Second Hall snapshot their departure-triggered forced effects; the first player orders multiple sources. Goblin physically returns itself and the prior top encounter discard without being destroyed, and Hall reveals a complete encounter card.

Dark Pit is an active-location Refresh action. It selects one to three distinct owned characters able to exhaust, pays all exhaustion costs, then discards the actual available top player-deck cards. Their combined printed costs must be strictly greater than Bane's remaining hit points. X or absent printed costs contribute zero. A successful Pit discard wins at stage three without requiring quest progress; ordinary final-stage progress cannot win while Bane remains. Hand, deck, attachment ownership and physical discard identities are preserved.

## Verification and interpretations

`tests/shadow-flame.test.ts` covers every original design and shadow, normal/easy setup for one to four players, actual multiplayer defense/player attacks, selected-player Feint, cost-preserving Counter cancellation, late threat, elimination, nested immediate combat, zero-hit-point interruption, refresh/Web timing, Dark Pit comparisons and saved choices/continuations. The combined scenario/resource/source-duration regression and ordinary/guided/hidden/decisions complete-game runs are recorded in the local validation logs. Campaign-only simulations are skipped because the supported campaign covers Core chapters.

`node --import tsx scripts/browser-shadow-flame.mjs` passes at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels with no page or console errors. The proof exercises one physical staged Bane, disabled optional engagement, two fellowships' normal defenses/fresh shadows and own attacks, saved combat, actual combat-to-Refresh regeneration, the Pit's equal-cost failure and greater-cost victory after saved selections, and the printed final quest reverse. Screenshots are in `output/shadow-flame`. These are local validation results.

The fresh-shadow source decision is stated above. No scenario-specific published ruling was found for nested Bane combat backups, an eliminated fellowship's retained attack ledger, the current-quest attachment case in Whip Lash or snapshot ordering after simultaneous character departures. Those cases follow the printed instructions and general rules, with explicit regressions. The implementation uses no inferred destruction trigger for Ranging Goblin or Dark Pit.
