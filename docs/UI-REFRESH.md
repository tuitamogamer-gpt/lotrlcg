# Interface refresh

The table keeps the forest, parchment, and gold identity while making decisions and card information easier to reach. Comfortable and compact density, large card hover previews, contextual play from the inspector, hand sorting and playable filtering, discard-pile browsing, keyboard shortcuts, and a mobile action dock are client-side presentation features. They do not alter the rules engine.

New original scenery was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then copied into the project as optimized JPEGs. The original generated PNGs remain untouched.

- `public/art/anduin.jpg`: river mission artwork.
- `public/art/dol-guldur.jpg`: fortress mission artwork.

## Anduin prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original cinematic landscape background for a Lord of the Rings solo card game mission selection screen. Scene: A majestic broad river winding between ancient forested cliffs, distant misty mountains, pale dawn catching the water, weathered stone outcrops and reeds in the foreground. Muted sage green, luminous silver water, restrained warm amber sunrise. Style: sophisticated hand painted high fantasy environment concept art, painterly brushwork with believable geological and botanical detail, quiet dramatic atmosphere, layered depth. Composition: wide landscape 16:9, important scenery toward the right half; left third is quiet, darker scenery suitable for an overlaid interface title. Beautiful detailed readable natural light. No characters, no text, no lettering, no symbols, no logos, no interface, no playing cards, no watermark.
