# The Blood of Gondor player scripts

All ten player designs (06107–06116) use their exact catalog definitions and are registered for automated play. This does not register the pack's encounter cards or quest.

| Card                  | Implemented behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Caldara               | Choose the actual eligible Spirit allies in your discard pile. Discard Caldara as the cost; put allies into play without playing them. The count uses only the other heroes you control with printed Spirit icons. Persistent markers enforce the once-per-game errata after revival and phase/round cleanup. Uniqueness is checked for the full selected batch.                                                                                                        |
| Squire of the Citadel | Reuses the existing optional leaves-play response in `gondor-player-cards.ts`, including discard costs and other players' eligible Gondor heroes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Tome of Atanatar      | Printed Leadership hero count reduces the play cost. Attach to an eligible Leadership hero, including a hero with a gained icon. Choose an actual Leadership event in the controlling player's discard pile, legal targets, explicit X for Men of the West and a complete normal resource payment. Discard the Tome only after these choices. The physical event stays outside discard while resolving and finishes on the owner's deck bottom, including cancellation. |
| Guthlaf               | Reuses the existing global Rohan-hero cost reduction and Gondor-hero sentinel condition in `rohan-player-cards.ts`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| The Hammer-stroke     | Encounter action engages all eligible staging enemies and enemies engaged with other players, regardless of engagement cost. It preserves movement prohibitions, immunity and Pippin's prevention. Enemies already engaged with you are not engaged a second time. Ordinary engagement triggers still fire; this is not an optional engagement.                                                                                                                         |
| Emery                 | Separate action in hand with a full three-card discard cost. Choose any active player to control her. The acting player's deck pays the cost; Emery retains her physical owner. Resolve the sphere-dependent discard before deck-discard responses. Ordinary resource-paid play does not use this action.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Children of the Sea   | Choose a Silvan or Noldor ally you control. Grant +2 willpower until phase end, then shuffle that same still-in-play ally into its physical owner's deck. The delayed effect survives source resolution and control changes but does not follow a new instance that left and reentered play.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Anborn                | Exhaust Anborn to recover the actual selected Trap attachment from your own discard pile, preserving ordinary exhaustion prohibitions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Poisoned Stakes       | Play the physical attachment into staging without a target. All unblanked waiting copies attach to the next eligible entering enemy; immune enemies and enemies that cannot have attachments are skipped. Each unblanked attached copy deals 2 damage at round end. Enemy defeat discards each Trap to its owner.                                                                                                                                                       |
| Well-Equipped         | Discard up to the available top two cards as an effect. Offer only attachments actually discarded by this resolution. Choose an eligible Dwarf character anywhere at the table, respecting attachment restrictions and unique titles, or decline. Preserve the attachment's indexed physical identity and owner. Resolve attachment entry before deck-discard responses; the attachment is put into play, not played.                                                   |

The module exports ordinary play, cost, action, hand-action, special attachment entry, staging entry, effect, phase-end and round-end hooks. Generic engine integration snapshots Children of the Sea's delayed effects before clearing phase markers. A temporary ally receives a saved first-player choice between Sneak Attack's hand return and Children's deck shuffle; either departure resolves once and keeps physical ownership. Poisoned Stakes' passive damage and resulting defeat resolve before Forced round-end payment windows and round continuation. All pending choices and effects use serializable state.

The source catalog is `src/data/official-player-cards.json` (RingsDB provenance retained in the ten-card data file). The official [FAQ 1.9](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19_printer_friendly.pdf) supplies Caldara's once-per-game errata. Fantasy Flight's [FAQ announcement](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2017/11/15/a-map-for-your-journeys/) explains the change. The [official product](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/products/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game/products/the-blood-of-gondor/) and [pack rules](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/ffg_content/lotr-lcg/support/rulesheets/rulesheet-the-blood-of-gondor.pdf) identify this expansion; quest rules remain outside this player-card implementation.

Validation: `node --import tsx --test tests/blood-gondor-player-cards.test.ts` passes 29 semantic tests covering all ten designs, restrictions, multiplayer control/ownership, revival, costs, cancellation, delayed effects, physical duplicate selection and saved choices. `scripts/browser-blood-gondor.mjs` passed Caldara, Emery, Tome, Poisoned Stakes and both Children/Sneak Attack departure choices through real UI and save/reload at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels. It produced 27 screenshots with no console/page errors, horizontal overflow or clipped dialogs. The result is recorded in `output/blood-gondor/report.json`.
