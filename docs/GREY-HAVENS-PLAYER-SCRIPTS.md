# The Grey Havens player cards

All 15 original player designs, 11001–11015, are registered as scripted cards. Definitions preserve the official catalog text, OCTGN identity and original printed art. The [RingsDB pack](https://ringsdb.com/set/TGH) is the transcription source; the card faces and original Grey Havens rules determine gameplay.

| Cards                                                      | Implemented behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Círdan the Shipwright                                      | Adds one resource-phase card and requires a discard from the physical cards just drawn; blocked draws cannot discard an older hand card.                                                                                                                                  |
| Galdor of the Havens                                       | Optional setup replacement of an arbitrary subset replaces the mulligan; declining retains the ordinary mulligan. His empty-hand Action draws six once per game.                                                                                                          |
| Mithlond Sea-watcher, Warden of the Havens, Sailor of Lune | Read their controller's current discard-top card; stats, ranged, sentinel and quest damage protection update continuously. Blanking removes the printed abilities.                                                                                                        |
| Skyward Volley                                             | Combat Action pays the event and one controlled ranged character's exhaustion, then assigns each repeated two-damage effect to an engaged enemy.                                                                                                                          |
| Anchor Watch                                               | Attack-declaration Response pays once and declares exhausted controlled defenders, including multiple defenders without Stand Together. Normal defender restrictions and declaration responses apply.                                                                     |
| The Evening Star, Elwing's Flight                          | Resolve one effect plus one for each older copy in the initiating player's discard pile; each repetition chooses a legal target. The resolving event remains outside the discard pile until its whole ability finishes.                                                   |
| Grappling Hook                                             | Discards its physical attachment and exhausts its character to commit during the quest phase. That character contributes attack instead of the ordinary quest stat through the phase.                                                                                     |
| Mariner's Compass                                          | Beginning-of-travel Response exhausts both attachment and host, searches at most five encounter cards, switches a chosen location with a staging location, and shuffles. Neither face of an Uncharted island can be switched into the encounter deck; stale targets are rejected before any deck changes. The switched-in card is neither revealed nor travelled to.                                       |
| Lindon Navigator                                           | Commits without exhausting and can commit while exhausted. After a quest resolves, its controller must discard it or a discardable hand card.                                                                                                                             |
| Explorer's Almanac                                         | Attaches only to staging locations. After active locations buffer successful quest progress, the first player may allocate progress among Almanac locations or continue to the current quest. Ordinary player-effect progress is unaffected.                              |
| To the Sea, to the Sea!                                    | Exhausts the attachment and discards an exact physical subset. The next Noldor ally actually played this phase receives the discount, to a minimum of one; putting an ally into play does not consume it.                                                                 |
| Narya                                                      | Attaches only to Círdan or Gandalf and continuously grants the Leadership resource icon. Its Action exhausts attachment and host, readies up to two distinct allies, and grants each +1 attack and defense through the phase. A ready ally can still receive the bonuses. |

Player choices and interrupted resolutions use serializable queued effects. Physical hand cards, event copies, attachment owners, initiating controllers and per-game limits survive reloads. Lasting phase bonuses use the existing phase cleanup, while discard-top abilities are recalculated from live state.

`tests/grey-havens-player.test.ts` checks registration, timing, resource and exhaustion costs, controller versus physical owner, repeated effects, attachment targets, immunity, forced choices, phase cleanup and reload boundaries. `scripts/browser-grey-havens-players.mjs` exercises real paid plays, ability buttons, target dialogs, repeated choices, resource draw and save restoration at desktop and mobile widths; it checks original card images and writes screenshots and a machine-readable report to `output/grey-havens-players/`.
