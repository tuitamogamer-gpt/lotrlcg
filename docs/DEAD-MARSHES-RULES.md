# The Dead Marshes rules support

The original scenario is scripted in normal and easy mode. The separate Nightmare recipe remains reference content until its additional rules are implemented.

## Primary sources

- [FFG original rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/b5/9b/b59ba9ef-dfbb-47dd-a0e3-8353dc21167f/the_dead_marshes_rulesheet.pdf): difficulty 5; The Dead Marshes, Sauron's Reach and Wilderlands encounter sets. Escape commitment exhausts characters, with an explicit action/event window before cards are dealt. A single-player test uses that player's characters; the party test allows every player. Only Escape values apply to dealt cards; unprinted Escape is zero. Success requires strictly greater strength. Dealt cards are discarded after resolution. A failed capture resets to stage 1B without repeating setup, preserves active-location progress and leaves other cards unchanged.
- [FFG Easy Mode](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_easy_mode.pdf): set aside two each of A Wisp of Pale Sheen, The Lights of the Dead, Giant Marsh Worm, Impassable Bog and The Heart of the Marshes. Shared Core sets remove three Sauron's Reach cards and four Wilderlands cards. After moving Gollum to staging, the initial deck has 53 normal cards or 36 easy cards; setup then reveals one per player.
- [FFG Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/90/19/90191e4e-a341-4379-b398-5963b7a87ebf/mec01_online_only_rules_reference_for_website.pdf): **Player Elimination**, **Modifiers**, **In Player Order**, **Guarded**, **Victory Display**, **Encounter Deck**, **Response** and **Timing of Ability Resolution** provide the common rules. The current player count and Gollum token count are evaluated when escape cards are dealt after the action window. This timing is an application of the rulesheet and current game state, not a card-specific FAQ ruling. Eliminated players contribute neither characters nor a dealt card to a party test.
- [FFG FAQ 1.9](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19_printer_friendly.pdf): no separate original Dead Marshes escape ruling overrides the rulesheet. Quest commitment and escape commitment are different instructions: Sacked prohibits the former, while We Do Not Sleep changes only quest exhaustion.

## Printed card coverage

The canonical encounter fixture contains all nine designs and the quest fixture contains both double-sided quest cards. Card codes, printed values, quantities, images and product provenance are retained from the imported catalogue. The rulesheet and printed card text govern automation.

Gollum starts in staging without a guard. At eight resource tokens he immediately leaves for the shuffled encounter deck, losing those tokens; if subsequently revealed he re-enters with zero. If merely dealt for escape or shadow, he follows that discard route. At each quest end the party attempts his one-card-per-player test; a failure adds two tokens. Defeating The Capture offers the first player a choice of the single player who attempts the final test. Gollum must still be in play for a capture to win.

Wisp offers any player's ready Lore hero, including a granted Lore icon, to reduce its two-token effect to one. Fens and Mires adds one token after travel. Bog counts staging locations including itself and grants seven victory points when explored. Every staged or engaged Worm heals two at round end. An active Heart increases each dealt card's Escape by one, including cards without a printed Escape. Nightfall and Mist test the first player and raise all players' threat after failure; Mist compares attack. Lights performs a separate test for each player and raises only that failing player's threat. All treachery threat is an encounter effect, allowing applicable Elfhelm responses.

## Resolution and verification

Escape tests preserve their original continuation separately from the ordinary effect queue. Characters chosen for escape are exhausted without setting or clearing their quest flags and without initiating quest-commitment responses. Their current stat values are used after actions finish; a departed character contributes zero, and a readied selected character cannot be selected twice. Events, abilities and their responses resolve during the explicit escape action window. Adventure progression remains paused until the player resolves the test. Setup escape tests have the same printed action window before opening hands are kept.

An initiating treachery remains outside the encounter discard pile until its embedded tests finish. Likewise, cards already dealt for a test stay outside the discard pile until that test ends. This prevents either group from being prematurely reshuffled into an empty encounter deck during the same resolution. The ordinary quest-phase encounter refill still applies when the deck empties.

`tests/dead-marshes.test.ts` verifies 1–4-player normal/easy recipe quantities, setup reveals, current modified stats, ties and zero-card cases, every new encounter effect, both quest stages, guard-free Gollum recovery, failed-capture board preservation, cross-player actions, cancellation, suspended continuations, save/reload and elimination during an escape action window. Whole-game starter simulations and browser interaction checks are maintained separately by the integration task.
