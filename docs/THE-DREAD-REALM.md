# The Dread Realm

Original Angmar Awakened adventure, standard and easy modes, one through four players. The source bundle is `src/data/pending/the-dread-realm-import.json`; its 24 definitions and 28 original faces include the two-sided rules reference. Runtime data adds 12 encounter definitions and three quests, and reuses eight canonical Lost Realm encounter definitions. The reference rules card is excluded from physical setup pools: the normal encounter deck contains 46 cards, the easy deck 29, and Daechanar plus Altar of Midwinter are set aside separately. The printed easy-mode resources follow the shared setup system.

The three stages follow the original card backs. Stage 1 requires 18 progress and transfers its attached Sorcery cards to stage 2B. Stage 2 cannot advance through progress: Daechanar must be defeated. Stage 3 redirects progress on the main quest to staging locations after active-location buffering. A chosen side quest still receives its own progress. At the end of each quest phase, stage 3 deals one damage to every character if any location remains in play, or wins if none remain.

The original rules reference at `/cards/74f12c0b-6c63-4b2e-a15f-41b179b9dec9.jpg` defines Reanimated Dead. Reanimation preserves the physical player card and its owner, hides its printed identity, and places a rules-defined Undead enemy engaged with the specified player as though it just engaged from staging. Its engagement cost is zero; threat, attack, defense and hit points are each two. Every leave-play destination is its owner's discard pile. Possession explicitly moves the newly reanimated ally into staging. The synthetic rules definition adds no physical encounter card to either recipe.

Daechanar counts Sorcery cards in play, including an attached Wraith of Carn Dûm. Each positive damage event is canceled in full while a Sorcery card remains, and removes exactly one chosen Sorcery from the game. Removal does not trigger Possession's discarded-only text. Daechanar also gains Indestructible while a Sorcery remains; Altar of Midwinter cannot receive any progress while he is in play.

Wraith of Carn Dûm changes from an enemy to a Sorcery attachment when it leaves play, preserving its physical identity. It returns to staging as an enemy when its attached character leaves play. Witch of Angmar resolves the When Revealed text of each Sorcery shadow after that card's shadow effects, without resolving reveal-only keywords or treating the card as a new encounter reveal. Attaching a Sorcery shadow moves that actual card out of the enemy's shadow pile. Sinister Dungeon similarly moves the explored location into the new enemy's shadow pile and resolves the When Revealed effects of Sorceries it discards from the encounter deck.

Player-deck discards resolve one physical card at a time, allowing immediate discarded-card responses before the next discard. Terror of the North counts the distinct printed types across the complete multiplayer batch. Tombs of Carn Dûm reanimates each actual ally it discarded, rather than substituting an older matching copy. Power of Angmar retains the shared distinction between protected player-card effects, permitted pure costs, and encounter effects. This adventure has no printed player-deck exhaustion loss condition.

Semantic and save tests are in `tests/dread-realm.test.ts`. They cover both setup recipes at all four player counts, hidden physical ownership, stage transitions, progress buffering and escape, Daechanar's replacements, Wraith/Possession zone changes, Witch shadows, Dungeon, multiplayer discards and malformed saves. Source provenance is local original card faces and the Dragncards catalog and scenario recipes; the pending bundle's generic Lost Realm expansion-insert URL is not presented as a Dread Realm-specific rules source.
