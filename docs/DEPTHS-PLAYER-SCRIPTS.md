# The Thing in the Depths player cards

All nine original player designs, 12030–12038 (25 physical cards), are scripted. The import preserves original printed names, rules, OCTGN identities and pack provenance from the [original RingsDB pack](https://ringsdb.com/set/TitD). All nine original faces are cached locally. Raiment of War reuses its existing complete passive and attachment rules.

| Card                | Implemented rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lanwyn              | Actual encounter revelation with printed, gained or conditional Surge opens an optional ready/+2 willpower Response. The physical hero may respond twice per phase; declining spends no activation. Readying restrictions apply, and the lasting willpower bonus survives later blanking until normal phase cleanup.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Eldahir             | Any player can initiate the Action using one spendable Lore resource from their own effective hero pool. They choose an individual facedown shadow on an engaged enemy, including another player's enemy. The card is looked at after actual payment and mandatory cost responses, remains facedown, and its shadow is not resolved. A real shadow effect grants two defense until phase end. The once-per-phase limit follows physical Eldahir, independent of the initiating player.                                                                                                   |
| Captain's Wisdom    | Only a Resource Action. Exhausting a controlled Noble hero is an additional event cost and happens before cancellation. The chosen hero gains two resources if still eligible; resource gain restrictions and effective Sword-thain heroes are respected.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Master Ironsmith    | Actual paid PLAY from hand records the resource pools used, including automatic payment and effective Sword-thain heroes. A single positive hero pool permits the optional Response after ally entry; split, zero, free and put-into-play entry do not. The paying hero remains the exact physical target through Forage/Carn reservation, gained Doomed and reload. A legal Weapon or Armor from the controller's hand or discard is attached without paying its resource cost, while ownership, physical identity, target restrictions, uniqueness and Restricted slots are preserved. |
| Raiment of War      | Attaches to a Warrior character, occupies two Restricted slots and grants one attack, one defense and two hit points. Normal plays pay its Tactics resource cost; extra Restricted attachments use the existing physical discard choices.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Sam Gamgee          | Controlling any Frodo Baggins character reduces the printed resource cost by two, including a blanked Frodo. An actual positive threat increase permits that player to pay one Spirit resource from their own hero pool to ready physical Sam and grant one willpower, attack and defense until round end. Already-ready Sam still receives the stat bonus. A physical once-per-round limit survives controller changes; the lasting bonus survives blanking and phase changes.                                                                                                          |
| Elevenses           | A Quest Action only after the staging step. The player selects physical Hobbit characters they control that are currently questing, then readies and removes those characters from the quest. Only a fully resolved pre-Then effect reduces that player's threat by the selected count. Choices retain physical IDs across reload, recheck targets before mutation and leave unselected questers unchanged.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Mirkwood Explorer   | Each physical, committed Explorer can respond to an actual successful quest by storing one progress token on itself. Its Action exhausts it, then moves its progress to an eligible location in play through normal progress/exploration rules. Existing immunity, cannot-place-progress and progress-capacity restrictions apply; progress that cannot move is retained on the ally.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Guarded Ceaselessly | Attaches to an eligible location. The attachment's controller exhausts a Ranger or Scout they control as the Action's cost; after mandatory cost responses, the location gets minus two threat for the phase. The attachment itself does not exhaust and has no usage limit. Repeated reductions stack and normal threat calculation floors the result at zero.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

Costs, optional Responses and continuing selections use serialized effect queues and existing player-ability boundaries. Mandatory responses to resource spending and exhaustion finish before the effects those costs purchased. Phase and round markers use the existing lifecycle cleanup. Rule checks use current traits, effective hero type, legal sphere icons, physical ownership and attachment controller. Actual departures clear the departed instance's limits and lasting effects, including same-ID Dread Realm reanimation; control changes preserve them.

`tests/depths-player.test.ts` provides 38 focused semantic checks, including actual plays and Actions, source records, single/split/automatic/free/zero payments, effective-hero resource pools, cancellation, remote ownership, phase/round limits, normal exploration and saved continuations. Independent `tests/depths-integration.test.ts` exercises encounter timing and delayed paid-entry interactions against actual engine hooks.

`scripts/browser-depths-players.mjs` exercises every design with real controls at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels. The full suite has 105 UI checkpoints and three artwork checks covering all nine original faces in each context. It reloads unresolved selections, checks dialog bounds, visible image decoding, horizontal overflow, browser errors and the expected deployed entry asset. Screenshots and a machine-readable report are written to `output/depths-players/`. `GAME_URL` selects a frozen local or canonical production deployment; `EXPECTED_ENTRY` and `RELEASE_SHA` bind the evidence to a release. Optional `DEPTHS_BROWSER_SMOKE=1` is a single-width diagnostic and does not replace the full release proof.
