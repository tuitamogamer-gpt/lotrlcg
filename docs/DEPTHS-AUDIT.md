# The Thing in the Depths independent rules audit

This audit covers the original standard and easy scenario. It checks the printed rules independently of the scenario implementation and adds engine-level regressions in `tests/depths-audit.test.ts`. Nightmare and the revised Dream-chaser campaign are outside this import.

## Primary sources

The original FFG MEC49 insert is available at [mec49_rules.pdf](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mec49_rules.pdf). The independently downloaded copy, `output/mec49-independent-rules.pdf`, has SHA256 `48c07078e3cb7f1b99edd5f79a2840957fa07c159d3ebe6423e5e18fa180435d`. Its text is cached at `output/mec49-independent-rules.txt`.

The original card fronts and quest backs supply clauses absent from the insert. The catalog transcriptions are a search aid; cached scans must establish the final wording. The boss front has no Massive keyword. Its attacks use the printed Eye of the Beast and Lashing Tentacles instructions, with a new shadow for each attack.

The current publisher page links the original [FAQ v1.9](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19.pdf), independently cached at `output/depths-official-faq-19.pdf` with SHA256 `a82b0e7e1fe27d942eaeae9426e6821ccc8157dad9a424e70d2f5bafa604e7d9`. Clause 1.42 states: “When an enemy makes an additional attack, discard all of its previously dealt shadow cards before dealing it a new shadow card.” Accordingly, Eye-triggered boss attacks discard Quarter Deck's previously dealt card before drawing their fresh shadow. The discarded physical copy must already be available in the encounter discard at the first Eye attack; it cannot also remain suspended for later restoration.

The independently verified original [Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/90/19/90191e4e-a341-4379-b398-5963b7a87ebf/mec01_online_only_rules_reference_for_website.pdf), cached at `output/lotr-rules-reference.pdf`, has SHA256 `7f47c746c21b93c8c65a5da62b9a6f8e878955519b1e610e0a86b05fa96f5aea`. Its Delayed Effects and Simultaneous Effects entries require delayed instructions to resolve at their specified future point, with the first player choosing the order of simultaneous effects. FAQ 1.02 specifically illustrates this with delayed Sneak Attack and Beorn effects. Giant Arm and Lashing Tentacles therefore receive a new ordering choice after attack damage, even when their shadows were resolved in a different order. Saved choices retain the actual pending Giant Arm identities.

The original Forecastle Deck scan (`public/cards/5571b159-9889-4fcd-bc0e-13bea7443348.jpg`) contains a shadow clause omitted by the catalog: +1 attack, or +2 if the defending character has no resources on it or in its resource pool. The promoted card restores this printed clause. A hero's pool and an objective ally's resource tokens both satisfy the check; an undefended attack has no defending character and receives the ordinary +1.

FAQ 1.09 and 1.37 require Forced effects to resolve before optional Responses to the same occurrence. Destroying a Creature with Secret Vigil must therefore resolve the boss's damage before offering Secret Vigil or attack-destruction Responses. If that Forced damage destroys the boss, the scenario wins immediately and no optional Response is offered first.

The boss's printed “+3 hit points per player” is a continuous modifier expressed in words. FAQ 1.43 requires continuous variable modifiers to recalculate; the Rules Reference's Player Elimination entry says elimination leaves one fewer player in the game. Applying those rules, eliminating a middle seat in a three-player game reduces the boss from 17 to 14 health while both required Elite allies remain in play. This follows the general rules; the FAQ does not provide a separate ruling naming this boss. The fixed starting-group calculation for the later Hunt for the Dreadnaught per-player symbol does not appear on this original card.

## Independent regression targets

The audit tests target the interactions most likely to escape isolated card tests: printed versus current Grapple criteria; physical identity and save validation across attachment and detachment; remote-seat attack attribution; nested and delayed shadow attacks; transition removal from hidden shadow zones; active-location threat loss; immunity versus encounter destruction damage; and snapshots used when Creature threat modifiers disappear as it leaves play. Pending choices must survive a JSON save and restore without exposing facedown encounter information in the public state.

The 16 independent semantic regressions save and restore pending choices before resolving them, and exercise actual attacks, travel and quest transitions. The audit found fixes for direct engagement/discard of a grappled physical enemy, required-objective loss during controller elimination, an indestructible Captain at lethal damage blocking later elimination checks, simultaneous delayed shadow ordering, and the boss's immediate discard of previous shadows. It also verifies both copies of repeated Giant/Grasping shadows, the retained later shadow cursor, continuous boss health after middle-seat elimination, same-action victory when that health becomes lethal, Forced damage before optional destruction Responses, and the restored Forecastle clause across hero and objective-ally defenders.

Validation command: `node --import tsx --test tests/depths-audit.test.ts` under Node 22. Broader scenario, player, browser and release verification are recorded separately in the release report.
