# The Steward’s Fear: original and easy scripted adventure

The complete original and easy recipes use the 18 encounter designs and three quest designs from The Steward’s Fear, the five shared Streets of Gondor designs, and the four shared Brigands designs implemented by the Heirs of Númenor module. Original/easy recipe quantities remain 51/45 cards before setup separates the hidden pools. Nightmare content is outside these recipes.

The printed definitions are preserved in the dedicated data files. The scenario handlers, shared Streets handlers, and pure save validator live in `steward-fear.ts` and `steward-fear-support.ts`. Registry and framework integrations expose the adventure through the normal game controls.

## Rules and source decisions

- [Official FFG adventure rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/79/22/7922597e-bbe3-4b27-9d06-292491f17cd9/mec18-the-stewards-fear-rulesheet.pdf): separate Underworld deck, randomly selected hidden Plot and Villain, Clues, and Underworld entry/departure rules.
- [Official FFG FAQ 1.9](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19_printer_friendly.pdf), especially 1.02, 1.18, 1.22, 1.37 and the False Lead clarification: simultaneous effects, exploration, reveal versus put into play, trigger/cancellation timing, and ending the quest phase immediately.
- The local official catalog supplies printed text, card identities, quantities, easy omissions and all quest sides. Quests one and two have no printed numerical progress threshold; four resource tokens advance them. The final stage requires 15 progress and no Villain in play.

Underworld cards attach face down to the actual location when it enters play, including a location put into play without revelation. When that location leaves play by any method, its underlying cards reveal one at a time. This is a scenario rule and still operates after the location’s printed text is blanked. The Underworld deck never refills. Cards discarded from it enter the encounter discard pile. Revealing from Underworld preserves a distinct origin, so encounter-deck-only effects such as Thalin and Castamir do not trigger.

Chosen hidden cards and cards beneath locations remain private in the public state projection and browser presentation. Counts are visible. Separate hidden pools, pending revelations, actual location IDs, selected costs, physical event identities, and pending choices survive JSON save/reload. The save validator rejects illegal hidden codes, impossible source quantities, unknown location hosts and malformed token counts.

## Printed behavior

| Design                          | Implemented behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Quest 1                         | Active-location exploration adds one resource token; progress cannot advance the quest; four tokens reveal the chosen Plot and advance.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Quest 2                         | Roots of Mindolluin becomes active; previous active locations return to staging with their facedown cards intact. Four new tokens advance.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Quest 3                         | Reveal the chosen Villain and shuffle the remaining Underworld deck into the encounter deck. Cards beneath locations remain beneath them. Win requires 15 progress and the Villain defeated.                                                                                                                     |
| The Fourth Star                 | Underworld equal to player count; on its exploration each player separately accepts or declines drawing one card.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Sewers                          | Own Underworld 1 and explicit choice of active location for the additional facedown card.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Houses of the Dead              | Underworld 2; exhaust every fellowship’s characters whenever it becomes active.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Storehouse                      | Underworld 1; shadow adds one attack, or two if undefended.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Roots of Mindolluin             | Underworld 1; each staging location receives two additional quest points.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Underworld Dissident            | Additional attack for each current Underworld location; undefended shadow removes one current quest token, including final-stage progress. If both token types exist, choose explicitly.                                                                                                                         |
| A Prisoner / A Scrap of History | Exhaust a controlled ready hero; add the Clue to victory; add two/one quest resources.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Secret Map                      | Exhaust a hero to attach the actual objective; a separate action moves that physical attachment to victory and places three progress on a legal chosen active location. No automatic quest resources or Restricted slot.                                                                                         |
| Up in Flames                    | At round end add a resource to the Plot, discard that many cards from each deck, and lose if any player’s deck is empty.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Poisoned Counsels               | Each player’s threat increases by two at round end, alongside the regular refresh framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Unholy Alliance                 | Reveal one additional staging card; lose if an enemy attack destroys a hero. Ordinary discarding does not meet that condition.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Daughter of Berúthiel           | Cannot optionally engage. After an actual attack its engaged player chooses return to staging or pays four threat to prevent the return. Prevented attacks do not offer this choice.                                                                                                                             |
| The Hand of Castamir            | After attacking, reveal and resolve a real encounter card, then discard that same revealed card. Non-exploration location departure still reveals Underworld cards. Encounter-deck treacheries cause an immediate first-player attack even when their When Revealed is canceled.                                 |
| Telemnar’s Bane                 | Every attack discards three actual cards from every player deck. Actual event plays from hand, Tome/discard replay, Vilya, and response-event windows trigger one immediate first-player attack. Put into play is distinct. Mandatory attack resolution preserves pending physical events and their destination. |
| A Knife in the Back             | Randomly select a first-player ally, snapshot its current attack, explicitly choose that player’s damaged hero, then discard that ally. Surge if no ally was discarded.                                                                                                                                          |
| Unwelcome Discovery             | Printed Surge plus one Underworld revelation; a Clue is discarded instead of staged.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| False Lead                      | End the quest phase immediately, canceling remaining staging and success/failure resolution. Outside the quest phase it does not move the phase. Shadow clears all current quest resource/progress tokens only after an attack destroys a character.                                                             |
| City Street                     | Printed Surge; restrict travel to other City Street copies while its text is active in staging. Shadow adds two attack.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Local Trouble                   | Attach to an eligible hero with the highest printed threat, with first-player tie choice. Charge the current controller for actual exhaustion, readiness and accepted printed hero ability triggers; offers, declines, attachment abilities and resource losses are distinct.                                    |
| Lost in the City                | Cannot cancel its When Revealed; each player searches an actual City location from encounter deck/discard and puts it into play, then shuffles. Its shadow discards the defender’s hand.                                                                                                                         |
| Market Square                   | Immune to player effects while unblanked; each player explicitly spends one legal hero resource before travel. Orc Vanguard restrictions apply to spending. Its shadow discards all defending hero resources.                                                                                                    |
| Pickpocket                      | On actual attack explicitly discard one hero resource and a random physical hand card, resolving what is possible. Defended shadow chooses a controlled attachment; undefended shadow discards all controlled attachments. Discarding resources is distinct from spending.                                       |

Shared Streets effects work in the Heirs recipes as well. Lieutenant’s repeated When Revealed does not create another discarded treachery copy; repeated Local Trouble moves the existing discarded physical card into its Condition attachment.

## Validation

`node --import tsx --test tests/steward-fear.test.ts`: 42 public-engine semantic tests, including original/easy setup for one through four players, hidden-zone conservation, blanking, simultaneous active locations, all shadows, multiplayer costs/control, pending save/reload, mandatory event timing, and actual cancellation.

`npm run check` verifies the shared TypeScript integrations. The combined Blood/Steward/discard-event regression run passed 76 tests.

`scripts/browser-steward-fear.mjs` drives the real interface at 1280×900, 390×844 and 320×750: Map claim/use and Plot reveal, Local Trouble hero/attachment actions, per-player Market payments, deck/discard City searches, both Daughter outcomes, final Villain victory and Up in Flames loss. Each pending choice is reloaded before resolution. The completed browser run covers 24 flows and 36 screenshots in `output/steward-fear/report.json`. The script also checks hidden-state presentation, visible art loading, horizontal overflow, dialog geometry, and browser errors. It defaults to port 5178 and accepts `GAME_URL`.
