# A Journey to Rhosgobel rules support

The original scenario is automated in normal and easy mode. Its Nightmare variant remains imported reference content with separate rules.

## Primary sources

- [FFG original rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/a-journey-to-rhosgobel-rules.pdf), linked from the [current official product support page](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/product/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-card-game/). This confirms difficulty 6, the Rhosgobel/Spiders of Mirkwood/Dol Guldur Orcs sets, Wilyador's ordinary ally control, first-player transfer each round, prohibition on player attachments, loss on any departure or controller elimination, and the separate removed-from-game zone.
- [FFG Easy Mode](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_easy_mode.pdf): remove three Exhaustion, three Swarming Insects and one Festering Wounds. The shared Core sets additionally remove five Dol Guldur Orcs cards and four Spiders of Mirkwood cards. The final encounter deck contains 53 normal cards or 37 easy cards after Wilyador and Rhosgobel are removed for setup.
- [FFG FAQ 1.9](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/lotr_faq_19_printer_friendly.pdf), p. 10: Return to Rhosgobel heals five wounds separately for each controlled Athelas. Sections 1.01 and 1.22 distinguish encounter-deck reveals from cards merely added to staging, so Forest Grove's searched objective does not gain an encounter guard. Section 1.23 treats claimed objectives as attachments.
- [FFG Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/90/19/90191e4e-a341-4379-b398-5963b7a87ebf/mec01_online_only_rules_reference_for_website.pdf), **Cannot** and **Attachment Cards**: an absolute healing prohibition remains in force; an attachment is discarded when its bearer leaves play. **Response** and **Timing of Ability Resolution** govern immediate exploration responses. **Cancel** confirms that canceled damage cannot trigger an ability requiring damage actually dealt.
- Printed original definitions and physical normal/easy setup recipes are preserved in the pinned sources documented in [CONTENT-IMPORT.md](CONTENT-IMPORT.md). The quest metadata uses the exact reverse-face image associated with each imported UUID.

## Implemented rules

- All nine original encounter designs and three double-sided quest stages are available. Setup places Rhosgobel in staging and gives the first player Wilyador with two damage. Rhosgobel's threat equals the number of players. Wilyador can quest, attack and defend as an ordinary Eagle ally; he transfers with the first-player token while keeping his physical identity and damage.
- If Wilyador leaves play for any reason or his controlling fellowship is eliminated, the entire party loses immediately. He suffers two damage at each round's end. Player attachment target lists, direct play and attachment movement reject him.
- Stage one needs eight quest progress. Rhosgobel cannot be traveled to before completing that stage, though player effects can explore it while staged. While Rhosgobel remains staged, Wilyador cannot be healed by any effect, including the final quest. Moving it to the active slot or exploring it removes that restriction.
- Every single effect heals at most five damage on Wilyador. During stage two, a successful heal removes its actual source card from the game: hero, ally, attachment or resolving event. A resolving event changes its own physical copy's destination to removed, preserving any older identical copy already in discard. Zero healing removes no source. Removed cards go to their owner's removed zone, do not trigger destruction responses and cannot be recovered from discard. Ordinary leave-play responses still apply.
- At stage two's quest start, the first player may place damage equal to player count on Wilyador to inspect the top three encounter cards. The chosen card is revealed normally, including Guarded, Surge, Doomed and cancellation windows; the others are discarded after the chosen reveal finishes. Paying lethal damage loses immediately before the lookup. Skipping costs nothing.
- Stage two needs twelve quest progress. The final stage heals five damage separately for every Athelas controlled by the players. Each treatment's after-healing responses, including another player's Elrond, finish before the next treatment and the final outcome check. Wilyador's complete healing wins; any remaining damage loses. Unclaimed staging objectives do not count. Pending exploration responses finish before the final treatment, and there is no new action window to claim a just-searched objective before that treatment.
- Revealed Athelas gains an encounter guard. A ready hero exhausts to claim an unguarded copy, without any threat cost. A claimed copy is an attachment: when its hero leaves play, Athelas goes to encounter discard. Forest Grove optionally searches the encounter deck or discard for one copy and adds it to staging unguarded, then shuffles the deck.
- Black Forest Bats and Mirkwood Flock allow only Eagle characters or characters with Ranged to attack or defend against them. Their shadows send undefended attack damage to Wilyador instead of a hero.
- Festering Wounds deals two damage to every wounded character; its shadow deals one, or two for an undefended attack. Exhaustion deals two damage to every exhausted character, with one from its shadow. Swarming Insects deals one damage to characters with no physical attachments; its shadow deals three to the uniquely most-damaged character, including Wilyador. Ties cause no additional damage. These effects cover all fellowships.

## Verification

`node --import tsx --test tests/rhosgobel.test.ts` covers exact normal/easy recipes for 1–4 players, quest faces, all stage transitions and win/loss conditions, first-player control and saves, healing prohibition/cap/source removal, real player healing actions, attachment prohibition, guarded objectives and search recovery, exploration timing, multiplayer treacheries, the optional quest-start response, combat restrictions and every new shadow effect.

Three additional final-treatment regressions cover separate Elrond responses, optional decline, cross-player ownership, save/reload between treatments and the absence of a response when Rhosgobel prevents healing. This sequencing derives from the FAQ's separate-treatment ruling and the Rules Reference's Response entry. No published Rhosgobel-plus-Elrond-specific ruling was found; it is documented as a general-rule interpretation.
