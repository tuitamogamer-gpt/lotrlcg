# Road to Rivendell rules support

The module implements the original scenario's twelve encounter designs and three double-sided quests. Normal and easy recipes use Road to Rivendell, Misty Mountains and Plundering Goblins: 56 and 45 encounter cards, respectively. Nightmare remains reference content.

## Sources

- [FFG original rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/de/85/de8570d9-d959-4612-b954-cbceacc01f6d/road-to-rivendell-rulesheet.pdf): difficulty four; controlled Arwen follows the first-player token; her departure or controller's elimination loses the game. Other Arwen copies cannot enter by any means. Ambush checks the newly entering enemy against players in first-player order and stops when it engages.
- [FFG Easy Mode](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/ffg_content/lotr-lcg/support/easy-mode/LOTR_Easy_Mode.pdf): physical easy quantities are retained from the matching original scenario recipe, including the two shared encounter sets.
- [FFG FAQ 1.9](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/1d/bb/1dbb319c-6b1e-466e-9177-75a5857b5cfa/lotr_faq_19_printer_friendly.pdf): sections 1.18 and 1.19 cover immediate exploration and setup effects; 1.26 and 1.42 cover outside-combat shadow cleanup and discarding old shadows before an additional attack; 1.35 distinguishes entering play from moving an existing enemy to staging; 1.37 establishes passive, forced and response timing.
- [FFG Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/90/19/90191e4e-a341-4379-b398-5963b7a87ebf/mec01_online_only_rules_reference_for_website.pdf): simultaneous effects, damage assignment/placement, replacement effects, restrictions, ownership/control and the dependency of a post-Then clause on its preceding clause.
- [Reproduced designer answers](https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/): §3.101 explicitly discusses Orc Raiders' full two-attachment requirement; §3.119 gives Caleb's generic ruling that an “each” clause requires at least one affected target. The latter applies to Sleeping Sentry's empty target set by interpretation. This compilation is a secondary source; these are not presented as published FFG FAQ entries.

## Implemented rules

Arwen is installed before setup revelations. She behaves as a controlled ally, can choose another player's hero for her exhaustion resource response and retains damage and attachments when control passes with the first-player token. Her loss is checked during setup and before optional leaving-play responses.

Ambush applies when an enemy enters play, including a return from discard. Moving an existing enemy back to staging does not trigger it. Barren Hills suppresses the keyword while active. Goblin Gate tracks the first enemy revealed each round, including one killed immediately by Thalin. Its passive immediate attack precedes an enemy's After-engagement forced ability. First-revealed identity remains tracked if the Gate becomes active later that round; applying its later engagement clause in this case follows its constant wording.

Immediate attacks retain the current phase and pending revelation/quest effects. They allow a defender choice without an ordinary player action window beforehand. Each gets a new shadow after discarding old shadows. They preserve the enemy's ordinary-attack flag. Outside combat, their shadows are discarded after the attack; during combat they remain until normal cleanup. Feint, Thicket of Spears, Forest Snare and other supported attack prohibitions prevent the attack.

Taskmaster damage and Raider discard clauses require an in-play, unblanked source when they initiate. Raiders selects two controlled attachments before discarding them together and leaves a lone attachment untouched. Orc Ambush completes simultaneous engagement moves before triggered followups, and the first player orders multiple pending encounter effects.

Followed by Night includes controlled objective allies in its damage branch, and its attack branch permits first-player ordering across engaged enemies from all fellowships. Sleeping Sentry deals damage before exhausting surviving ready characters, waits for supported damage decisions and suppresses exhaustion if Frodo cancels any of that damage. Its shadow discards the defending player's exhausted characters, including an exhausted defender; this is discard rather than destruction.

Pathless Country removes one token from an incomplete placement. A placement reaching its five quest points immediately explores it, so its After ability cannot initiate from discard. Ruined Road's optional response follows actual travel, and its ready option only includes first-player heroes. Return-to-staging shadows trigger normal added-to-staging responses without restarting Ambush.

Quest thresholds are twenty, seven and thirteen. Orc Outpost searches both encounter deck and discard for Goblin Gate; the location becomes active only if the search succeeds and there is no active location. Approaching Rivendell reveals one encounter per player and forbids character healing. The prohibition also applies to Beorn's Hospitality and healing abilities that bypass the generic heal effect.

## Verification and rule interpretation

`tests/road-rivendell.test.ts` has 37 focused tests. The live checkout passes those tests and all 28 Redhorn tests. The combined original expansion scenario regression passes 249 tests across Carrock, Emyn Muil, Rhosgobel, Dead Marshes, Return to Mirkwood, Khazad-dûm, Redhorn and Road. Arwen's title restriction is verified through normal play, direct ally entry, Sneak Attack, Stand and Fight and Timely Aid. `npm run check` passes. Complete seeded ordinary/guided/hidden/decisions simulations pass for Redhorn and Road; their two campaign-only cases are skipped because campaign support currently covers the Core Set chapters. No deployment is implied by these local results.

`node --import tsx scripts/browser-dwarrowdelf-scenarios.mjs` passes at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels with no page or console errors. It exercises an actual Goblin Gate immediate attack, the Elrohir response during that attack, save/reload while combat is pending, preservation of the ordinary attack flag, outside-combat shadow cleanup, persisted first-revealed identity/round, the final healing prohibition and the printed quest reverse. Screenshots are retained in `output/dwarrowdelf-scenarios`.

Sleeping Sentry with Dori or Song of Mocking damage redirection has no explicit scenario-specific primary ruling in the consulted sources. The implementation adopts the Rules Reference reading that a successful replacement changes how the original damage effect resolves, while actual cancellation prevents that effect from resolving. It therefore treats successfully redirected damage as a resolved damage effect and detects actual Frodo cancellation separately. Dori, reciprocal Songs, and Song-to-Frodo cancellation each have focused regression coverage. Interpreting the pre-Then clause as requiring placement on every original character would instead suppress exhaustion after a redirect. That alternative would need a per-damage outcome hook; aggregate target damage snapshots cannot distinguish separate redirected damage instances. The chosen interpretation derives from the general replacement/Then entries rather than a Sentry-specific ruling.
