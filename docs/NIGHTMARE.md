# Nightmare mode

A Nightmare Deck turns a scenario into its Nightmare variant: its setup card
lists the encounter cards it removes from the scenario's standard encounter
deck, its own encounter cards are shuffled into the rest, and the setup card
is flipped next to the quest deck, its rules active for the whole game.
Nightmare mode is offered in the lobby (next to easy mode, which it replaces)
for each scenario whose Nightmare Deck is supported; the scenario archive's
Nightmare list of such a scenario offers it too ("Choose adventure"). It is
not offered in campaign mode.

**Supported so far:** the Core Set Nightmare Deck (Passage Through Mirkwood,
Journey Along the Anduin and Escape from Dol Guldur) and all six of Shadows of
Mirkwood's (The Hunt for Gollum, Conflict at the Carrock, A Journey to
Rhosgobel, The Hills of Emyn Muil, The Dead Marshes and Return to Mirkwood),
all three of Khazad-dûm's (Into the Pit, The Seventh Level and Flight from
Moria) and all six of Dwarrowdelf's (The Redhorn Gate, Road to Rivendell, The
Watcher in the Water, The Long Dark, Foundations of Stone and Shadow and
Flame), and all three of Heirs of Númenor's (Peril in Pelargir, Into
Ithilien and The Siege of Cair Andros), and Against the Shadow's The
Steward's Fear, The Drúadan Forest, Encounter at Amon Dîn, Assault on
Osgiliath, The Blood of Gondor and The Morgul Vale. The other Nightmare Decks
follow in publication order, beginning with The Hobbit: Over Hill and Under
Hill's.

## The framework

- `src/game/nightmare.ts` holds the supported Nightmare Decks (setup card and
  printed removals by title), builds the Nightmare encounter deck from the
  standard one, and gives the quest inspector its Nightmare card and lines.
- A game in Nightmare mode keeps `nightmareMode` (never with easy mode); the
  scenario's own setup then runs on the Nightmare encounter deck, and the
  setup card's setup rule follows.
- `src/game/nightmare-core.ts` (the Core Set), `src/game/nightmare-som.ts`
  (The Hunt for Gollum and Conflict at the Carrock) and
  `src/game/nightmare-rhosgobel.ts` (A Journey to Rhosgobel) and
  `src/game/nightmare-emyn.ts` (The Hills of Emyn Muil) and
  `src/game/nightmare-dead.ts` (The Dead Marshes) and
  `src/game/nightmare-rtm.ts` (Return to Mirkwood) and
  `src/game/nightmare-pit.ts` (Into the Pit) and
  `src/game/nightmare-seventh.ts` (The Seventh Level) and
  `src/game/nightmare-flight.ts` (Flight from Moria) and
  `src/game/nightmare-redhorn.ts` (The Redhorn Gate) and
  `src/game/nightmare-rivendell.ts` (Road to Rivendell) and
  `src/game/nightmare-watcher.ts` (The Watcher in the Water) and
  `src/game/nightmare-longdark.ts` (The Long Dark) and
  `src/game/nightmare-foundations.ts` (Foundations of Stone) and
  `src/game/nightmare-shadowflame.ts` (Shadow and Flame) and
  `src/game/nightmare-pelargir.ts` (Peril in Pelargir) and
  `src/game/nightmare-ithilien.ts` (Into Ithilien) and
  `src/game/nightmare-cairandros.ts` (The Siege of Cair Andros) and
  `src/game/nightmare-steward.ts` (The Steward's Fear) and
  `src/game/nightmare-druadan.ts` (The Drúadan Forest) and
  `src/game/nightmare-amondin.ts` (Encounter at Amon Dîn) and
  `src/game/nightmare-osgiliath.ts` (Assault on Osgiliath) and
  `src/game/nightmare-blood.ts` (The Blood of Gondor) and
  `src/game/nightmare-morgul.ts` (The Morgul Vale) script the setup
  cards' rules and the Nightmare
  encounter cards as a layer, chosen by scenario and composed with its hooks
  (the composition the Ered Mithrin Campaign Pool uses, which also composes
  the doomed and combat-phase hooks, the state check, the indestructible,
  attack and defense prohibitions a Nightmare layer adds), so the standard
  encounter cards keep their own scripts. Its lasting effects are saved with
  the game and checked when a save is restored; a Nightmare card's threat
  raise resolves in the affected player's own seat.
- A Nightmare Deck may add stages to the quest deck (Conflict at the
  Carrock's stage 3): the stage count a save accepts and the table shows
  follows it. It may also replace a stage (Into the Pit's Goblin Ambush, in
  place of Goblin Patrol): the table, the quest inspector and the quest's
  attachments follow the new stage, whose own revealing effects and defeat
  condition replace the printed stage's. Or it may add quest cards to a quest
  deck of routes (Flight from Moria's, in place of Blocked by Shadow): a
  route's face, quest points and victory come from its own card.
- The quest inspector shows the Nightmare card (its rules side first) and the
  rule it applied; the end-of-game log names the mode.
- The coverage audit counts a Nightmare recipe as automated once its
  scenario's Nightmare Deck is supported.
