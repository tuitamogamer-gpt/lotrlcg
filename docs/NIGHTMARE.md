# Nightmare mode

A Nightmare Deck turns a scenario into its Nightmare variant: its setup card
lists the encounter cards it removes from the scenario's standard encounter
deck, its own encounter cards are shuffled into the rest, and the setup card
is flipped next to the quest deck, its rules active for the whole game.
Nightmare mode is offered in the lobby (next to easy mode, which it replaces)
for each scenario whose Nightmare Deck is supported; the scenario archive's
Nightmare list of such a scenario offers it too ("Choose adventure"). It is
not offered in campaign mode.

**Supported so far:** the Core Set Nightmare Deck (Passage Through Mirkwood,
Journey Along the Anduin and Escape from Dol Guldur) and all six of Shadows of
Mirkwood's (The Hunt for Gollum, Conflict at the Carrock, A Journey to
Rhosgobel, The Hills of Emyn Muil, The Dead Marshes and Return to Mirkwood),
all three of Khazad-dûm's (Into the Pit, The Seventh Level and Flight from
Moria) and all six of Dwarrowdelf's (The Redhorn Gate, Road to Rivendell, The
Watcher in the Water, The Long Dark, Foundations of Stone and Shadow and
Flame), and all three of Heirs of Númenor's (Peril in Pelargir, Into
Ithilien and The Siege of Cair Andros), and Against the Shadow's The
Steward's Fear, The Drúadan Forest, Encounter at Amon Dîn, Assault on
Osgiliath, The Blood of Gondor and The Morgul Vale, and The Hobbit: Over Hill
and Under Hill's We Must Away, Ere Break of Day, Over the Misty Mountains
Grim and Dungeons Deep and Caverns Dim, and all three of The Hobbit: On the
Doorstep's (Flies and Spiders, The Lonely Mountain and The Battle of Five
Armies), and all three of The Voice of Isengard's (The Fords of Isen, To Catch
an Orc and Into Fangorn), and all six of The Ring-maker's (The Dunland Trap,
The Three Trials, Trouble in Tharbad, The Nîn-in-Eilph, Celebrimbor's Secret
and The Antlered Crown), and all three of The Black Riders' (A Shadow of the
Past, A Knife in the Dark and Flight to the Ford), and all three of The Road
Darkens' (The Ring Goes South, Journey in the Dark and Breaking of the
Fellowship), and all three of The Lost Realm's (Intruders in Chetwood, The
Weather Hills and Deadmen's Dike), and all three of The Treason of Saruman's
(The Uruk-hai, Helm's Deep and The Road to Isengard), and Angmar Awakened's
The Wastes of Eriador, Escape from Mount Gram and Across the Ettenmoors. The
other Nightmare Decks follow in publication order, beginning with Angmar
Awakened's The Treachery of Rhudaur.

  encounter cards keep their own scripts. Its lasting effects are saved with
  the game and checked when a save is restored; a Nightmare card's threat
  raise resolves in the affected player's own seat.

  Carrock's stage 3): the stage count a save accepts and the table shows
  follows it. It may also replace a stage (Into the Pit's Goblin Ambush, in
  place of Goblin Patrol): the table, the quest inspector and the quest's
  attachments follow the new stage, whose own revealing effects and defeat
  condition replace the printed stage's. Or it may add quest cards to a quest
  deck of routes (Flight from Moria's, in place of Blocked by Shadow): a
  route's face, quest points and victory come from its own card.
- The quest inspector shows the Nightmare card (its rules side first) and the
  rule it applied; the end-of-game log names the mode.
- The coverage audit counts a Nightmare recipe as automated once its
  scenario's Nightmare Deck is supported.
