# The Battle of Carn Dûm

Normal and Easy mode are scripted for one to four fellowships. The runtime uses the original double-sided Thaurdir, both quest cards, twelve encounter definitions from this pack, and the eight exact canonical Angmar Orcs / Dark Sorcery definitions from The Lost Realm. The insert remains a reference card, outside the encounter deck.

The printed faces and the [official Adventure Pack insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/d2/94/d294fc13-1186-4de4-938c-57b7d34ddeab/mec43_rules.pdf) are the rules authority. The immutable import is `src/data/pending/the-battle-of-carn-dum-import.json`; recipe snapshots are `src/data/carn-dum-recipes.json`. The [scenario guide](https://visionofthepalantir.com/2019/01/24/the-battle-of-carn-dum/) provides a secondary cross-check for the physical deck and exactly-three-shadow trigger. FAQ 3.202 orders a Sorcery treachery's When Revealed, Thaurdir's Forced effect, then Surge. FAQ 3.192 distinguishes cancellation of When Revealed from cancellation of the entire encounter card.

Three catalog discrepancies were corrected against original images: Furious Charge has **Surge**; Midwinter's Crux refers to **Midwinter's Crux**, rather than “Sever the Head,” in its fifteen-progress condition; and Thaurdir's reverse has the **Champion** trait. Imported source snapshots retain their original values.

## Setup and victory

Each opening hand is decided before encounter setup. Thaurdir enters staging Captain side faceup, one available Garrison per player enters staging, and one Accursed Battlefield becomes active. Unused setup Garrisons return to the encounter deck. Normal mode has 45/44/43/42 cards in the deck for one/two/three/four players. Easy mode has 29/28/27/27; its gold-ring removal leaves only three physical Garrisons, so four-player setup adds all three available copies. Removed Easy-mode cards are never recreated to pay setup.

Quest 1 requires fifteen main-quest progress, keeps Thaurdir in staging, and prohibits all damage to him. Unresolved enemy shadows persist through combat; resolved shadows discard after their attack. An exactly-three-shadow condition triggers once until the count or zone changes, preventing repeated flips when an attack is prevented. At round end Champion flips back to Captain.

Quest 2 raises **each** player's threat by the total shadows on enemies in play, then flips Captain to Champion. One physical Thaurdir stays in staging and is considered engaged with every active fellowship. Each fellowship receives its own normal attack, with a fresh shadow dealt when that attack begins. His printed Indestructible remains until the **main quest** has fifteen progress. Reaching fifteen alone does not win: Thaurdir must actually be defeated. At round end Captain flips to Champion; this attack resolves before old-round modifiers expire.

The insert says flipping keeps all tokens and attachments and does not leave play. Thaurdir's new face's Forced effect triggers after the flip. He can never enter the encounter deck because neither face has an encounter back. His printed attachment prohibition and Indestructible can be blanked; quest-card prohibitions remain.

After-attack effects use the first player's ordering decision when several are due together. Pending flips, additional attacks, damage assignments and searches are stored in the ordinary effect queue. Virtual engagement records each fellowship's normal attack without cloning the enemy. Saved Carn state validates round effects and exactly-three trigger identities.

## Verification

`node --import tsx --test tests/carn-dum.test.ts` passes 46 semantic cases covering physical recipes in all eight mode/player combinations, source corrections, malformed saves, Battle and side-quest progress, repeated defense penalties, both boss faces, shadow retention, Werewolf attack shadows, real multiplayer engagement, victory through real combat, every new encounter / shadow family and reused Sorcery rules. Cancellation tests cover revealed-card Forced effects after a canceled When Revealed and Hasty Stroke against a granted Mountains shadow. Round-end testing preserves Furious penalties during the pending attack. Separate Walls taxes allow Favor of the Valar to replace elimination between resource payments. `tests/carn-dum-fixtures.ts` also exports save-valid Captain, Champion and immediate-defense fixtures for browser validation.
