# The Three Trials and Trouble in Tharbad player cards

All twenty player designs (`08025`–`08034` and `08056`–`08065`) are registered. Eleven are newly implemented; the other nine reuse existing starter/Collector scripts. These two encounter scenarios and the Voice of Isengard quests remain pending.

The pinned RingsDB records in `public/catalog.json` supply printed identities, text, quantities and provenance. `src/data/trials-tharbad-player-cards.json` preserves the complete player-pack snapshot. All eleven new card faces are cached locally. Existing reprints keep their canonical identity and original product information.

Rivendell Scout and Elven Mail reuse the shared Secrecy/Sentinel/Restricted rules. Feigned Voices, Haldir of Lórien, O Lórien!, Galadhrim Minstrel, Lembas and Defender of the Naith reuse the Silvan/Noldor scripts. Pursuing the Enemy reuses the Collector implementation.

Warden's timing follows Caleb Grace's 9 June 2015 designer answer, reproduced as ruling **3.188** in the [community FAQ compilation](https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/). It explains that a location explored by this passive never reaches staging and does not resolve its keywords or When Revealed effect. Optional Doomed grants use the keyword timing documented in [Voice of Isengard](VOICE-ISENGARD-PLAYER-SCRIPTS.md). Merely mentioning a possible Doomed grant in ability text does not make a card a printed Doomed card for The Seeing-stone.

Borrowed non-character attachments retain separate controller and owner fields. Their removal, player elimination and delayed returns preserve the original discard/deck destination. A self-returning borrowed event goes to the hand specified by its own text and is still subject to Message's round-end return; events never enter play. A card switched into the recipient's deck by Gildor keeps its original owner through shuffling, reordering, drawing, deck discard and reload. Discards go to the originating deck's discard pile, including cards subsequently selected by Well-Equipped. These distinctions follow **Discard Pile**, **Event Cards**, **In Play and Out of Play**, **Ownership and Control**, and **Switch** in the [FFG Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/f2/87/f28704b2-5f25-4fd8-be7a-18d4a5d2c1c4/mec101_core_set_rules_reference_v10c-compressed.pdf).

Pending decisions, delayed returns, borrowed deck identities and engagement restrictions are serialized and validated. Existing shared timing boundaries in [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md) continue to apply, including the absence of a general simultaneous-response ordering editor.

Validation: `tests/trials-tharbad-player.test.ts` exercises public actions, exact physical copies, cross-seat choices, cancellation, immunity, Doomed/Gríma, printed sphere matching, saved choices, Trap control and normal end-round processing. `scripts/browser-trials-tharbad-players.mjs`, included in `npm run test:ring-maker-players`, exercises real payment/target controls, reloads and delayed returns at 1280/390/320px and decodes all eleven new faces. The existing expansion, Silvan and Collector suites cover the reused designs.
