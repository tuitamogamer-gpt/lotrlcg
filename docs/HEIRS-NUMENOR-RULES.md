# Heirs of Númenor original and easy quests

The original/easy versions of Peril in Pelargir, Into Ithilien and The Siege of Cair Andros use twelve printed quest definitions and 46 encounter designs. `heirs-numenor-encounter-cards.json` owns 41 definitions; the five Streets of Gondor definitions are shared with The Steward's Fear. Nightmare versions remain catalog-only.

## Printed sources and errata

- [FFG Heirs of Númenor rules](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/c6/92/c692afb2-f6fa-4521-bc59-9c09e3883a39/heirs-of-numenor_rules.pdf): Battle, Siege, cumulative Archery from enemies anywhere in play, staging objective allies, damage on locations, and removed quest stages.
- [FFG FAQ 1.9](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/2e/31/2e3129b3-dc51-4c27-81ed-6a72f13e82f3/lotr_faq_19.pdf): Blocking Wargs errata; The Power of Mordor removes its former “Then”; encounter lasting modifiers recalculate with the game state; sources of Forced abilities must be active when their triggers occur.
- [FFG official easy-mode removals](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/16/2e/162ec99a-90fd-4c5f-8fac-6a0e6058f4aa/lotr_easy_mode.pdf): mode-specific encounter quantities and normal setup remain separate.
- Printed quest faces were checked against the original card images. The source catalog omitted Battle on the first two Peril backs and the Archery clause on Southron Counter-attack; the runtime definitions restore those clauses.

Top-of-deck discards invoke Storehouse once per actual location. Collateral Damage holds its resolving source outside the discard pile, counts existing copies after the initial two discards and preserves this behavior when Lieutenant repeats only its printed When Revealed effect. Searches and putting cards into play do not repeat When Revealed or Doomed/Surge; entry Forced abilities still trigger. Extra shadows from Orc Arsonist survive the transition to the combat framework deal.

## Validation

`node --import tsx --test tests/heirs-numenor.test.ts` passes 74 public-engine regressions, including normal/easy setup for 1–4 players on all three quests, Scourge numeric-string/X costs, and Power's physical source outside its replacement reveals. The 303 existing regressions for the twelve modules touched by the spending audit pass. TypeScript passes.

`GAME_URL=http://127.0.0.1:5203 node --import tsx scripts/browser-heirs-numenor.mjs` exercises actual client controls and save/reload for Scroll exhaustion and stage search, a Ranger's Trail action, shared Archery allocation, and Siege's single undefended damage packet. It covers 1280, 390 and 320-pixel layouts with reduced motion and screenshots in `output/heirs-numenor/`. The script's normal default is port 5178.
