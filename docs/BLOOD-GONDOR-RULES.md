# The Blood of Gondor original/easy rules

Runtime identity: `the-blood-of-gondor`. Recipes `Q03.8` and `E03.8` supply 38 and 29 encounter cards including setup cards. Fifteen encounter designs and both quest stages have native handlers. The Ravaging Orcs set reuses the Heirs of Númenor implementation. Nightmare remains separately gated.

Sources: the imported printed fronts/backs and recipes, [FFG rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/24/d9/24d94f36-9629-46cc-9101-ce2f3354afbe/rulesheet-the-blood-of-gondor.pdf), and [FAQ 1.9](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/2e/31/2e3129b3-dc51-4c27-81ed-6a72f13e82f3/lotr_faq_19.pdf). The FAQ's hidden-card clarification turns only the original group: cards subsequently gained through Evil Crow stay hidden. The corrected stage-one combat choice allows taking a hidden card even when the player has none.

Setup places The Cross-roads and Black Númenorean in staging, gives both objective allies to the first player and reveals one encounter per player. Hidden cards have physical identities in separate player piles. Taking a hidden card reshuffles an empty encounter deck even outside questing. Public state, choice labels and reviews show only counts until a card is turned faceup. Elimination discards that player's pile.

At each quest phase, every player takes one hidden card. Stage one's combat choice resolves before Archery and normal shadow dealing. Turning an enemy engages the pile's player; ordinary locations and treacheries are discarded. Turning is not revealing and does not trigger Surge, Doomed or Thalin. Lying in Wait additionally places locations and executes printed When Revealed effects. Faramir selects a physical hidden card belonging to any player and deals three damage to an enemy; Alcaron returns an enemy engaged with his controller to staging.

Stage two places the two objective allies under Captured! without a leave-play event and turns each player's current pile. They remain in play, with their identities and attachments, but cannot be controlled or activated. Five hidden cards force another turn. Cross-roads grants Siege while present; otherwise Captured! uses Battle. Active-location buffering applies before the final fifteen-progress victory.

Other handlers cover variable Black Númenorean threat and end-round threat, Evil Crow's new hidden card, Orc Ambusher's non-objective ally discard, Brutal Uruk's post-attack turning and fresh shadows, road travel costs/staging quest bonuses, Dark Woods' Archery and mandatory hidden discard, Conflict's phase-lasting engaged Orc threat, and every printed shadow effect. New enemies exposed by Uruk still receive their normal attacks before player combat begins.

Evidence: `tests/blood-gondor.test.ts` checks exact recipes, setup, hidden privacy, the FAQ snapshot, turning versus revelation, ownership, pending saves, travel costs, capture, objective loss, Archery/attack timing and victory. `npm run test:against-shadow-finale` exercises hidden-card selection, reload, faceup damage and the visible counter at 1280/390/320px. All new fronts and quest backs are cached under `public/cards/`.
