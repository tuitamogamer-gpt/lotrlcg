# The City of Corsairs player cards

The original pack contains nine player designs, 12138–12146, representing 25 physical cards. `src/data/city-player-cards.json` preserves the original catalog records without rewriting printed text, quantities, identities, or provenance. Original English JPEGs are bundled for all nine cards. See [the source audit](CITY-SOURCES.md).

| Card                      | Automated rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Prince Imrahil            | His controller may spend one resource from his pool during combat, once per round. The serialized search exposes eligible allies among the actual top five cards, using his effective traits and the normal uniqueness/scenario entry restrictions. A chosen ally enters without being played or paying its resource cost; the remaining deck is shuffled. At combat phase end, the exact surviving instance is removed from its current controller, attachments are discarded, and the card is shuffled into its owner's deck. Leaving play earlier ends that instance's delayed return. Declining to find an ally still shuffles the deck. |
| Knight of the White Tower | The existing single-pool payment rule is now exercised by this registered original card. A split payment is rejected even when its total is sufficient. Imrahil may put the ally into play without paying its resource cost.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Hold the Line             | Any player holding the event may respond when an enemy is declared as an attacker. The physical zero-cost Leadership event is played and can be canceled. The attacked player chooses up to three eligible defenders, including another player's Sentinel characters. The response applies to this attack only. The group may then pay two Tactics resources from one or more eligible pools. At the attack's completion, each surviving defender that took no actual damage from that attack readies. Pre-existing damage does not prevent readying; healing during the attack does not erase damage already taken.                         |
| Soldier of Dol Amroth     | Each actual entry offers its controller an optional Response, including entry through Imrahil or another effect. Each accepted Response reduces the next Tactics card played that phase by one; multiple Responses stack. A non-Tactics play leaves the discount available, a zero-cost Tactics play consumes it, and putting a card into play does not consume it. Source departure does not end the lasting discount.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Súlien                    | Any player may trigger the shared ally's action. That player chooses one of their own spendable Lore pools, pays one resource, and exhausts the physical ally. Only after successful exhaustion does each affected staging location receive minus one threat for the phase. Active locations and immune locations are excluded. The lasting modifier remains if Súlien leaves play.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Inspiring Presence        | The acting player chooses an eligible hero they control. Heroes across all living players with a strictly lower printed threat cost receive plus two defense for the phase. If the preceding instruction resolves in full, the players may spend two Leadership resources as a group to grant the corresponding attack bonus. Each allocation is explicit, supports shared payment, and survives a pending save. Equal threat costs do not qualify.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Guardian of Ithilien      | Each entry offers an optional return of an eligible enemy engaged with the ally's controller. The same physical enemy moves to staging, its shadows are discarded, and normal staging-entry effects are notified. Immunity, movement restrictions, and facedown Orc Guard replacement are respected. Another player's engaged enemies cannot be chosen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Ranger Spear              | Attaches to a Ranger character and occupies one Restricted slot. It grants one attack ordinarily and one additional point when that character actually attacks an enemy with an attachment. The extra point follows the actual enemy in combat and the attack preview; it does not increase unrelated attacks or general character statistics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Prince of Dol Amroth      | Attaches to a supported Prince Imrahil printing and grants the Outlands trait. If the bearer is a hero, the Title's controller must control Outlands allies in all four player spheres to gain one extra resource during actual resource collection. Allies controlled by other players than the Title's controller, neutral allies, promoted heroes, and blanked Title text do not satisfy the extra collection.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

Hold the Line's printed sequence is interpreted as declaring defenders, offering its optional group payment, then resolving the attack with the resulting delayed ready effect. This is an implementation interpretation of the original scan, not an attributed designer ruling. FAQ 1.04 supplies the existing rule for combining defense and assigning all combat damage to one defender. Actual damage is recorded independently of later healing and includes damage from that attack's shadows; canceled damage is not recorded.

All choices use normal serializable effects and player-ability boundaries. Resource and exhaustion reactions finish before their purchased effects. Group allocations use the shared resource-payment operation so each payer's mandatory scenario reactions occur once for that complete cost. The Imrahil return and phase modifiers follow their physical targets across control changes and do not require the initiating source to remain in play.

Validation is in `tests/city-player.test.ts` and `tests/city-hold-line.test.ts`. The initial focused run passed 42 tests, including actual play and combat actions, saved pending searches and group costs, one to four player ownership, strict printed threat comparison, phase expiry, source departure, cancellation, combined defense, damage history, and physical card conservation. `scripts/browser-city-players.mjs` and its Hold the Line fragment verify all nine original faces and actual client controls at 1280, 390, and 320 pixels.
