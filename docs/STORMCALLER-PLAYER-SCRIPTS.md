# Flight of the Stormcaller player cards

All 11 original player designs, 12001–12011 (31 physical cards), are scripted. The import retains original names, printed rules, OCTGN identities and cached original card faces. Definitions come from the [original RingsDB pack](https://ringsdb.com/set/FotS); the original faces determine the printed rules. In Service of the Steward and Azain Silverbeard reuse their existing rule implementations.

| Card                      | Implemented rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Denethor                  | Setup adds two resources once before scenario setup. His once-per-round Action transfers one resource to another eligible Gondor hero, including another player's hero; transfer is neither spending nor ordinary resource gain.                                                                                                                              |
| Rod of the Steward        | Attaches to a Gondor hero. Its controller spends two resources from that specific hero to draw one card. The Action does not exhaust anything and has no usage limit. Attachment control follows the host; physical ownership stays unchanged.                                                                                                                |
| In Service of the Steward | Continuously grants Gondor to its attached character. The existing passive supplies attachment legality and trait-dependent effects, including Denethor and Rod.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Azain Silverbeard         | Participating in a killing attack opens an optional Response for any player. A group payer spends one Tactics resource; another eligible enemy sharing a current trait with the destroyed enemy's last known traits takes two damage. Ability attribution follows the player who initiates it.                                                                |
| Last Stand                | A physical event can respond to an actual Warrior defender's destruction, including shadow-effect destruction. The attacking enemy takes damage equal to the destroyed character's printed attack. Discarding a defender does not trigger it.                                                                                                                 |
| Glorfindel                | Can be played from the initiating player's own discard with normal costs, timing and uniqueness. His Action discards a physical hand card to ready him once per phase.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Tides of Fate             | Responds only to an actual shadow-caused attack increase, including deferred choices and resource-driven enemy stats. The chosen current defender gets three defense for that attack. Optional group payment of two Tactics resources readies it and grants three attack for its next attack that phase; ordinary stats and Battle questing remain unchanged. |
| Imladris Caregiver        | Discards a physical hand card to heal one damage on an eligible character, including another player's character. No exhaustion is required. Each physical copy may act twice per round.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Heed the Dream            | The chosen player searches the top five cards, adds one physical card to their hand, then shuffles the whole deck. Only a successful first search permits the optional group payment of three Leadership resources and a second search of that player's entire deck. Search prohibitions apply before play, player selection and saved search continuations.  |
| Guardian of Rivendell     | Playing from hand requires two other, distinct physical hand cards. Choices reserve the costs without mutation; normal resources and both discards are paid together before discard responses. The original source and payment metadata remain reserved across save/reload. Putting it into play bypasses this printed PLAY cost.                             |
| Vanish from Sight         | Initiation requires control of two or fewer heroes, including a Sword-thain hero. Until phase end, that player's threat is treated as twenty when higher, even if hero control later changes. The stored counter, resource mutations, resets, score and save remain physical; rule comparisons use treated threat.                                            |

Choices, shared payments, paid event lifetimes and interrupted cost responses use serialized effects and existing pending physical-card reservations. Phase and round limits use the existing lifecycle cleanup. Player effects respect immunity, current traits, ownership, controller, sphere icons and active draw/search restrictions.

`tests/stormcaller-player.test.ts` contains 35 focused semantic checks covering actual plays and abilities, printed timing, physical costs, split payments, last known information, controller attribution, uniqueness, phase cleanup and save boundaries. Existing collector tests additionally cover the reused Service/Azain implementation; `tests/stormcaller-threat.test.ts` covers the treated-threat rule reads and actual counter operations. `scripts/browser-stormcaller-players.mjs` exercises all 11 designs through real controls at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels, reloads unresolved decisions, verifies original card art, and writes screenshots and a machine-readable report under `output/stormcaller-players/`.
