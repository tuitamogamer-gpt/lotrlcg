# A Storm on Cobas Haven player cards

The original pack's eleven player designs, 12109–12119, represent 31 physical cards. `cobas-player-cards.json` preserves the exact original RingsDB records, including printed text, OCTGN identities, pack quantities and provenance. Ioreth reuses the existing Collector's Edition handler; the other ten designs receive new automation. The eleven original English JPEGs are bundled locally. See [source audit](COBAS-SOURCES.md).

Na'asiyah remains a legal deck choice. When the objective-ally version enters during Temple of the Deceived or The Drowned Ruins setup, or is turned faceup in The Thing in the Depths, the unique encounter character takes precedence and the player version is discarded (Rules Reference, “Unique Cards,” p. 19). Its attachments go to their owners' discard piles, starting threat is preserved, and losing a player's final hero causes normal elimination. This discard does not count as destruction.

All pending choices use the normal serialized effect and player-ability boundaries. Paid costs and physical event destinations survive cancellation and save/reload. The delayed effects use physical character identities and explicit player ownership. Their duration still expires if the initiating player is eliminated.

Semantic checks are in `tests/cobas-player.test.ts`, `tests/cobas-reactive.test.ts`, `tests/cobas-economy.test.ts` and `tests/cobas-uniqueness.test.ts`. They exercise public play/combat actions, cross-player costs and control, phase and round expiry, payment origins, actual damage and attack outcomes, cancellation, physical ownership, and pending-save restoration. `scripts/browser-cobas-players.mjs` verifies the same cards through responsive client controls at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels; the final release's exact artifact and counts are recorded in [the release notes](COBAS.md).

The 2026-10-13 verification passed 79 focused player tests and 117 browser checkpoints (39 at each width), with no browser errors. Every original player face was inspected at each width. `output/cobas-players/report.json` records the loaded `index-CC0IUSyS.js` response: 313,494 bytes, SHA-256 `ee59fb6344dd44cd902dae165032ee45717edc2aaed766d94533084ded5f7e42`, and 117 screenshots. Separate focused regressions passed for damage replacement and the previous Dream-chaser scenarios.

After that full browser pass, one narrow correction allows a Healer promoted by Sword-thain and bearing Sacked! to exhaust as part of the Houses cost. Sacked! prevents the character's own ability but does not prevent paying another card's exhaustion cost.
