# The Sands of Harad player cards

The original deluxe contains thirteen player designs, 16001–16013, representing 35 physical cards. `src/data/harad-player-cards.json` preserves their original catalog identities, printed text, quantities and provenance. Greenwood Archer and Mirkwood Long-knife were already scripted through the original designs used by later starter decks; this import adds eleven new runtime definitions and exercises all thirteen originals. The Storm Comes receives its scan's missing numeric metadata at runtime: five quest points and Victory 1. The reference catalog remains unchanged.

All thirteen original English scans are bundled. The upstream image named `16006` is actually PNG data despite its `.jpg` source URL; the local file uses `.png` and preserves the original bytes. Other original player images use JPEG. Source hashes and card mappings are included in the [source audit](HARAD-SOURCES.md).

Gimli and Legolas have a separate identity bonus sentence, without a successful-ready “Then.” The implementation therefore permits choosing their named partner for that bonus even when the partner is already ready or cannot ready. Dwarven Shield's “damage from an enemy attack” is implemented as actual combat damage from that attack, including undefended combat damage; direct damage from a shadow is not combat damage. These are printed-text interpretations. No Harad-specific designer clarification was located in official FAQ 1.9; they are not attributed designer rulings.

The complete focused run passed all 44 tests in `tests/harad-player.test.ts` and `tests/harad-player-responses.test.ts`, with no failures or skips. It includes native play, commitment, engagement, defense and reveal actions, saved optional costs, one through four-player ownership, two consecutive Vigilant Dúnadan defenses, Hidden Cache before Erebor Guard entry, independent printed reveal keywords, real round expiry and physical card preservation.

`scripts/browser-harad-players.mjs` and `scripts/browser-harad-responses.mjs` define 38 client checkpoints at each of 1280, 390 and 320 pixels: all thirteen original faces plus actual response, payment, combat, quest and search controls. The desktop and 390-pixel Guard allocation dialogs and the 320-pixel Legolas hand-cost dialog were visually inspected.
