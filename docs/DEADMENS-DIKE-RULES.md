# Deadmen's Dike

`deadmens-dike` implements the third original Lost Realm scenario for 1–4 players in standard and easy mode. It adds eighteen encounter definitions and two quests, reuses Iârion, and preserves all twenty-three original local faces in the source bundle.

## Sources and interpretation

The [official Lost Realm insert](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/88/d6/88d6e80d-e75d-468f-8484-76c56b15e895/mec38_insert_web.pdf), original card faces and exact Q05.3/E05.3 recipes are recorded in the [import notes](DEADMENS-DIKE-IMPORT.md). Printed quest targets are 11/13. Thaurdir is the original single-face nine-hit-point enemy: Indestructible prevents damage from destroying him, and stage 2 requires any Thaurdir in play to have no remaining hit points before its progress target can win. Broken Battlements replaces one Time counter per player after its timeout.

Official FAQ sections 1.53–1.54 distinguish cancelling a Sorcery card's When Revealed effect from cancelling the entire encounter card. Cancelling only When Revealed still permits Thaurdir's post-revelation heal and attack; whole-card cancellation suppresses that trigger. Iârion's optional ready requires a side quest revealed from the encounter deck.

## Implemented behavior

| Mechanic                  | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup                     | Opening hands precede controlled Iârion, a fixed staged Fornost Square and physical set-aside Thaurdir. The standard/easy encounter recipes contain 41/28 cards. Stage 1B searches one Baleful Shade per player into direct engagement, shuffles, then reveals one card per player.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Main quests and depletion | The main stage remains active during side-quest selection. Stage 1 needs 11 progress and discards one top-deck card per player at refresh end. An empty player deck eliminates that player immediately under either quest stage, including depletion by draws and card effects. Other living players continue unless Iârion's departure or the last player's elimination loses the game.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Stage 2 and Thaurdir      | Advancement adds the original set-aside Thaurdir and reveals one encounter card per player. Victory needs 13 main progress and no Thaurdir in play with remaining hit points. Indestructible leaves him in play with lethal damage and does not grant immunity to player effects. Legal direct discard or return-to-hand effects can remove him; an absent Thaurdir supplies no hit-point blocker. He cannot take attachments, and heals three damage after an encounter-deck Sorcery revelation before making a real immediate attack against the first player. |
| Iârion                    | His willpower/attack/defense count main plus player/encounter side quests. Control follows the first player, including elimination. His optional ready follows side-quest revelation from the encounter deck. Ordinary departure loses immediately.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Side quests               | Physical current-quest selection preserves progress and attachments through reload. Seal the Tomb mills three encounter cards at refresh end, then returns the topmost Undead from the discard to staging. The Shadow World mills one player card per engaged Undead. The Power of Angmar blocks player-effect recovery from player discard piles; after defeat, its Forced effect shuffles each player's top five discard cards into that player's deck.                                                                                                        |
| Deck loss and Square      | Each actual top-player-deck discard retains physical cards, ownership and card types. A physical Fornost Square gains one resource for each qualifying first-player discard batch, and one threat per resource. Ordinary draws and discards from hand do not trigger it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Locations                 | Active Deadmen's Gate mills the triggering player's deck after player Action/Response effects. Haunted Keep gives Surge to the first revealed Undead each round while active. Battlements has per-card Time 3, mills five per player on timeout and receives one replacement counter per player; travel mills one per player. Norbury Tombs returns the topmost encounter-discard enemy as its travel cost and optionally shuffles each player's top five discard cards into that player's deck when explored as active.                                         |
| Undead                    | Cursed Dead returns every discarded copy to staging on revelation. Dead Lord engages the topmost discarded Undead with the same player. Baleful Shade mills the actual defending player's top card when it attacks and gains two attack if that card is an ally. Thaurdir's Damned mills two on engagement and assigns damage equal to their combined printed costs among that player's characters.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Sorcery and treacheries   | Unnatural Fog offers each player a committed-character-count mill or removal of that player's characters from the quest. Heavy Curse attaches to the physical current quest and increases a played card's cost when its owner's discard has a matching title. Dark Sorcery discards matching-title allies with the proper discard/destruction distinction. Terror counts distinct discarded card types for its round threat bonus. Restless Evil grants round bonuses to Undead or gains Surge when none is in play.                                             |
| Shadows and continuations | Printed shadows preserve defending-player scope, title/copy checks, discard versus destruction, attachment ownership, type counts, ready/exhaust restrictions and additional revelation. Stage-2 Thaurdir destruction offers its victim's controller three-card milling or return of Thaurdir to staging. Searches, damage assignments, immediate attacks and restoration choices survive save/reload.                                                                                                                                                           |

The interface shows every living player's deck count, Thaurdir's set-aside/in-play state, nine-hit-point damage and remaining-health objective, current quests, attached Conditions and Time. Saves preserve physical set-aside identity, discard ownership, Time, pending choices and scenario counters.
