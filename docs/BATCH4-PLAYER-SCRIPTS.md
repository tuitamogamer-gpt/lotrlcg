# The Black Serpent, Cirith Gurat, Poros and Rhovanion player cards

The four immutable player imports contain **45 original designs**: The Black Serpent `17081`–`17090`, The Dungeons of Cirith Gurat `17109`–`17118`, The Crossings of Poros `17135`–`17144` and The Wilds of Rhovanion `18001`–`18015`. Each JSON record is an exact copy from `src/data/official-player-cards.json` and `public/catalog.json`; the reference catalog is not rewritten. Later starter printings are not substituted.

All 45 original scans are cached under `public/cards/<code>.jpg`. Their original OCTGN identity selects `https://dragncards-lotrlcg.s3.amazonaws.com/cards/English/<octgnid>.jpg`. The release pins in `output/batch4-release/player-art-pins.json` record 45 URLs, paths, byte counts and SHA-256 values. Printed faces were visually read in eight contact sheets; Wilds 15 were independently reviewed by the Wilds implementer.

Printed corrections belong to the runtime overlay only: Rally the West has 6 quest points and Victory 1; Fierce Defense's second ability is a **Valour Combat Action**; Heirs of Eärendil restores its printed title; Followed says **If unattached**; Wind from the Sea's shadow is a separate shadow field; Bartering's cost definition restores the missing **X**. None of these corrections changes the original imports.

Existing complete rules are reused for Oath of Eorl and Riddermark Knight in `rohan-player-cards.ts`. Hauberk of Mail's attachment restriction, defense and Sentinel-dependent hit point were already native in `expansion-passives.ts`; the new Dale interactions use those same rules. No other existing registration is counted as a complete implementation.

The Wilds fifteen rules and Guarded(X) interactions are documented in `WILDS-PLAYER-SCRIPTS.md`. Brand, Bard, attachment discounts, dynamic keywords, saved transfers, King of Dale, Bow, Map, Necklace, Traffic, To Arms!, Valour of the North and Bartering are native mechanics.

The original [Poros rulesheet](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mec61_web_the_crossing_of_poros-rules.pdf) defines Encounter: up to 3 encounter-backed player cards are set aside separately from the 50-card player deck; their When Revealed cannot be cancelled; shadow copies go to encounter discard after an attack; copies leaving play are removed. Wind reserves and removed copies therefore occupy distinct saved state. The original [Rhovanion rules](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-fantasyflightgames/uploads/2026/09/mec65_web_the-wilds-of_rhovanion_rules.pdf), page 2, defines Guarded(X): discard until matching enemy/location, add without revealing, attach as guarded objective, and restore control to its owner with an eligible host choice when free. Cached PDFs/text in the ignored release directory preserve the consulted rules.

Native tests use real PLAY, ABILITY, COMMIT, DEFEND, ATTACK and encounter-reveal paths, serialize/reload at decisions, assert physical movement, costs, limits, controller scope and duration. The browser script exercises native controls and inspects every original face at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels, verifying decoded images and served bytes against the pinned scans. Reports bind script SHA-256 and the root-approved final build; a rehearsal does not serve as final artifact evidence.

Encounter reserve allocation is explicit in `createGame` options: `rangerReserves` and `batch4WindReserves` share a maximum of three cards per player. Without explicit options, a deck with Flight to the Sea alone reserves three Winds; a deck with both Flight and Ranger Summons reserves one Wind and two Rangers; other decks keep the existing three-Ranger default. Supplying only a Wind count assigns the remaining slots to Rangers; supplying only a Ranger count caps the default Wind allocation at the remaining capacity. Zero and mixed allocations persist independently for each seat. Removed Winds never replenish these setup reserves.
