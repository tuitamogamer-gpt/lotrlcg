# Assault on Osgiliath original/easy rules

Runtime identity: `assault-on-osgiliath`. Original recipe `Q03.7` contains 50 encounter cards; easy recipe `E03.7` contains 34. The only quest is Retake the City, with no quest-point victory. Nightmare remains separate and unimplemented.

The local catalog and recipes supply the 15 original encounter designs, one quest design and exact quantities. The Mordor Elite and Southrons encounter sets reuse the native Heirs of Númenor rules (Archery, Haradrim Elite entry attacks, attachment/resource restrictions, Mumak's round damage cap, repeated treachery text, sphere damage and encounter searches).

Primary rule sources:

- [FFG Assault on Osgiliath rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/3b/f9/3bf964fc-8ed2-4743-a3bb-168805b5451b/rulesheet-assault-on-osgiliath.pdf): encounter sets, controlled locations, resetting progress, active printed text and eliminated-player returns.
- [FFG FAQ 1.9](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/2e/31/2e3129b3-dc51-4c27-81ed-6a72f13e82f3/lotr_faq_19.pdf), page 4: Counter-attack erratum explicitly makes returning the highest-threat location or raising threat an either choice. The checked local text is `output/rules-audit/faq-1.9.txt`.
- The same FAQ, section 1.02: the first player orders simultaneous conflicting effects. The explicit choice between the active East Quarter and West Quarter stat replacements applies this general rule; it is an implementation interpretation, not a separate published ruling about these locations.
- [FFG Core Set Rules Reference](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/f2/87/f28704b2-5f25-4fd8-be7a-18d4a5d2c1c4/mec101_core_set_rules_reference_v10c-compressed.pdf), steps 6.4.1–6.4.3: choosing no defender lets the attack go undefended before shadow resolution and damage. If a defender leaves play before damage assignment, the attack becomes undefended at that point.

Setup gives every player an actual deck search for one enemy and one distinct available unique location. Cards are added rather than revealed. Forced entry attacks still resolve; ordinary When Revealed text does not. All chosen physical copies leave the encounter deck, which is shuffled after setup.

Explored Osgiliath locations move into a public controlled-location area, keep their physical identity and attachments, clear progress and become controlled by the first player. Captures from staging and direct printed actions follow the same rule. Locations remain in play, are available to card effects, have active printed text and contribute to per-controller encounter effects. Returning a controlled location clears progress and control and preserves its identity and attachments. Losing any location also returns that controller's unblanked Ruined Towers. Every character departure returns every controlled unblanked Ruined Square. Eliminated players return their locations to staging.

West Gate searches either encounter pile, reveals its selected Osgiliath location (including Surge), then becomes active. Using a controlled Gate loses control and triggers Towers. Ancient Harbor exhausts a hero belonging to the acting player during combat. Old Bridge pays resources from any combination of the acting player's legal hero pools in planning, at the printed one/two-resource cost. Ruined Tower can become a second active location after exhausting an eligible character from any fellowship to pay the group travel cost; both locations buffer quest progress with explicit allocation. King's Library resolves its encounter reveal travel cost before entering play as active. Harbor and Bridge cannot be travelled to. East/West Quarters provide battle/siege only while active and unblanked.

Counter-attack offers highest-threat ties separately and totals each player's controlled-location threat. Street Fighting cannot be canceled, discards until an Osgiliath location and lets only the first player exhaust a hero to claim it; adding or claiming the discarded card does not reveal it. Pinned Down snapshots the controlled count when resolved and adds that amount to the round's Archery total, with conditional Surge below four controlled locations. Southron Commander attacks each qualifying player from staging. Uruk Lieutenant adds the topmost Orc from discard without repeating When Revealed text. Southron Phalanx counts its engaged controller's locations. All printed shadows, casualty returns and unique-location undefended returns use the original defending player or the destroyed character's actual controller as printed.

The four unique locations return as soon as the attack goes undefended, so locations lost at defender declaration no longer count for that attack's Phalanx bonus or Soldier shadow. A defender destroyed by the attack's combat damage does not retroactively make a defended attack undefended.

Victory is checked at the end of the round after departures such as Gandalf, when the players control every Osgiliath location in play. Capturing the last staging location during planning or combat does not win early. Progress on Retake the City does not advance it.

`tests/assault-osgiliath.test.ts` exercises setup and exact recipes for one to four players, public actions, costs, control, multiplayer ownership, encounter and shadow effects, departures, elimination, timing and save validation. `scripts/browser-assault-osgiliath.mjs` exercises real setup decisions, Bridge capture/payment, controlled-location rendering and second-active Tower travel with reloads at 1280, 390 and 320 pixels. Browser artifacts are written under `output/assault-osgiliath/`.
