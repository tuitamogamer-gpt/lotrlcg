# The Weather Hills

`the-weather-hills` implements the second original Lost Realm scenario for 1–4 players in standard and easy mode. It adds seventeen encounter definitions and two quest stages, reusing the four shared Angmar Orcs definitions from Chetwood. The original bundle has 23 definitions and 26 local card faces.

## Sources and interpretation

The original card faces and [official Lost Realm rules](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/88/d6/88d6e80d-e75d-468f-8484-76c56b15e895/mec38_insert_web.pdf) define the separate Orc deck, Mission objective, encounter side quests and Time. [Import notes](WEATHER-HILLS-IMPORT.md) record the exact physical recipes and corrections to upstream transcription. In particular, Hunting the Orcs advances through its reverse face once, Cold from Angmar attaches independently of its damage instruction, and the set-aside location is Amon Forn.

[Collected Caleb Grace rulings](https://alongextendedparty.com/semi-official-errata-faq-1-10/), entries 3.132 and 3.270, clarify damage allocation and Cold: assignments cannot exceed a character's remaining hit points while another eligible character has room, and an already-resolving printed ability finishes even if its damage instruction causes Cold to blank the character. The [scenario guide](https://visionofthepalantir.com/2018/09/20/the-weather-hills/) supplies supporting context; its broader claim that every character death removes a Mission token is superseded by the printed reverse, which requires destruction by an enemy attack.

## Implemented behavior

| Mechanic                     | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup                        | Opening hands precede fixed active Exposed Ridge and staged Hunting the Orcs/Weathered Hilltop. Standard/easy main decks contain 31/24 cards; the separate Orc decks contain 11/9. Orc Ambush and Amon Forn retain physical set-aside identities. Fixed setup cards do not trigger revelation effects.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Stage 1                      | The first Weather treachery each round gains Surge. Exploring an active location reveals one Orc-deck card, or two with 3–4 players, as ordinary encounter revelation. Empty Orc decks never recycle. Progress does not advance the stage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Mission objective            | Each enemy defeat adds a resource and checks the threshold of 3 plus the number of players. At that threshold, Hunting the Orcs flips to Savage Counter-attack with all tokens, including any excess, intact and advances to stage 2A. Orc-camp's optional resource alone does not trigger this check. Each enemy attack that destroys a character removes one token from the reverse; zero tokens loses immediately.                                                      |
| Stage 2                      | Advancement ends the current phase, merges the remaining Orc deck and encounter discard into the main deck, then reveals the two set-aside cards. Damaged characters lose one willpower. At quest start, the first player chooses between removing a Mission token and revealing an extra encounter card. Victory needs 20 main progress and no Amon Forn in play.                                                                                                         |
| Encounter side quests        | The first player may choose them as the current quest. They retain independent progress, attachments and Time while main-stage text remains active. Their When Revealed effects cannot be canceled. Defeat adds them to victory and discards their attachments.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Find Shelter                 | Time 4 ticks at refresh end even when another quest is current. At zero, each player chooses one owned hero for four damage; the quest then receives four replacement counters.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Search the Ruins / Make Camp | Search increases every player's threat by two at refresh end; its optional defeat response reduces each by three. Make Camp prevents character healing while in play; its optional defeat response lets each player heal up to three from one owned hero after the restriction ends.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Weather and Conditions       | Hilltops gain tokens after Weather revelation and threat from those tokens. Ice Storm assigns damage per player, then exhausts all damaged characters. Biting Wind uses one shared committed-character pool. Freezing Blast removes damaged characters from the quest. Cold from Angmar attaches to the physical current quest and continuously blanks damaged characters' printed text except keywords and Traits. Healing or Condition removal restores those abilities. |
| Locations                    | Active Ridge damages one chosen character per player at refresh end. Ruins raises each player's threat after a canceled treachery. Ancient Causeway raises threat on exploration. Valley charges one Mission token to travel and optionally heals one character when explored as active. Orc-camp charges a physical encounter deck/discard Orc search to travel and optionally adds a Mission token when explored as active.                                              |
| Orcs and Amon Forn           | Cornered Orc attacks immediately after engagement. Active Amon Forn makes each Orc added to staging attack the first player with a fresh shadow, is immune to player card effects and blocks victory anywhere in play. Shared Angmar Orc, Marauder, Captain and Orc Ambush preserve discard/destruction distinctions, search scope and attack ordering.                                                                                                                    |
| Shadows                      | All printed shadows use the actual defending player, defender's damage, quest-card count and damaged-character count. Tragic Discovery offers three threat or an undefended attack. Damage assignments and additional attacks preserve their pending choices through reload.                                                                                                                                                                                               |

The interface shows the separate Orc-deck count, Mission tokens and reverse face, current-side-quest progress, quest Conditions and per-card Time. Save validation preserves the objective's face, set-aside physical identities, separate deck, Time and pending decisions.

## Verification

- Seventy-eight dedicated semantic cases pass. They cover 1–4-player standard/easy setup, both Mission faces, threshold timing and excess tokens, interrupted phases, separate-deck routing, every revelation and shadow, Time, healing restrictions, damage allocation, continuous blanking, side-quest defeat, multiplayer attack ordering, extra staging reveals and saved continuations.
- Forty complete Weather Hills games using the four Core learning decks terminate with valid saves. All forty lose under the simple test policy; semantic and browser fixtures separately verify victory. Hidden, decisions and guided review modes preserve complete-game outcomes.
- The Lost Realm fellowship sweep covers all 38 scenarios: 76 complete games and 38 matching decisions-mode runs. All games terminate under the same policy, with matching outcomes.
- The browser suite passes 78 checkpoints at 1280/390/320 pixels, including 24 actual 1–4-player standard/easy setup runs. It checks real menu mode selection, current-side-quest choice, Cold attachment/inspection/restoration, visibly contained quest Conditions, Time reset, Mission reversal, set-aside revelation, blocked and successful Amon Forn victory, last-token combat loss, review mode and reloads during choices. All 26 local faces decode at every width; browser errors, horizontal overflow and clipped dialogs are absent. Desktop opening/reverse/Condition views and mobile selection, Time and Condition views were visually reviewed.
- Type checking and the production build pass. The additive `202610080007_weather_hills_choices.sql` migration permits this scenario in account choices.

The dedicated hot-seat sweep completes 64 further games: 32 standard and 32 easy, across 1–4 players and eight seeds. Every action resumes from a valid JSON save. All games end in losses under the unchanged starter-deck policy; this verifies execution and reload consistency, rather than playing strength. The dedicated Weather Hills multiplayer CI job runs this sweep.

No hosted Supabase migration has been applied. Campaign and Nightmare variants remain reference-only.
